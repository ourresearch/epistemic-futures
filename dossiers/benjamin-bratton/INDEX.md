## Quick bio

Benjamin H. Bratton (b. 1968) is a philosopher of technology and Professor of Philosophy of Technology and Speculative Design at the University of California, San Diego, where he founded the Speculative Design undergraduate major (verified against his own site, https://bratton.info/, 2026). He directs **Antikythera**, a think tank on planetary computation and the evolution of intelligence that was founded in 2022 as an incubated project of the Berggruen Institute and became an independent non-profit in 2026 (https://antikythera.org/about, 2026); Antikythera publishes a book series and a peer-reviewed journal with MIT Press. Since 2024 he has also been a Visiting Faculty Researcher in Google's Paradigms of Intelligence group. He previously directed two research programs at Moscow's Strelka Institute — The New Normal (2017–2019) and The Terraforming (2019–2022) — succeeding Rem Koolhaas as program director; Strelka suspended activities in 2022. He holds a PhD in the sociology of technology from UC Santa Barbara and has taught at SCI-Arc, UCLA, the European Graduate School, and NYU Shanghai. His books include *The Stack: On Software and Sovereignty* (MIT Press 2016; tenth-anniversary edition 2026), *The Terraforming* (Strelka Press 2019), *The Revenge of the Real* (Verso 2021), *Dispute Plan to Prevent Future Luxury Constitution* (e-flux/Sternberg 2015), and *Accept All Cookies* (Berggruen Press 2025); he co-edited *The New Normal* and *Machine Decision is Not Final*. He writes regularly for Noema Magazine (Berggruen) and e-flux journal.
