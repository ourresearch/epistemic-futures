# The Terraforming (book)

## Publisher summary

The Terraforming (Strelka Press, 2019) is a concise manifesto for the research program of the same name that Bratton directed at the Strelka Institute, Moscow. Its argument, as described on his own site (bratton.info, retrieved 2026-08-13):

"The Terraforming (Strelka) is a concise manifesto for the research program of the same name, arguing that instead of retreating from the existential implications of the Anthropocene, planetary society must embrace anthropogenic artificiality in order to compose a planet that will sustain diverse life for the long term future, a terraforming not of Mars but of Earth."

A French translation, La Terraformation 2019, was published by Presses universitaires de Paris Nanterre in 2021 (OpenAlex W4417007251); its chapter-level records are listed in openalex-works-index.tsv and carry abstract-only baseline files in by/.
