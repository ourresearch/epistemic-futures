# The Terraforming

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

Among many things, the Terraforming urban design research programme at Strelka Institute in Moscow constitutes a polemic against dominant modes of planetarity, and the inadequacy in how critical philosophy and design seek to confront them. The terraforming programme considers the past and future role of cities as a planetary network by which humans occupy the Earth’s surface. Planetarity itself comes into focus through orbiting imagining and terrestrial modelling media (satellites, sensors, servers in sync) that have made it possible to measure climate change with any confidence. The terraforming programme remains one of ‘speculative urban futures’, but only because each of the three terms is undecided and therefore alive. The ‘speculative’ refers not to the whimsically ‘creative’, but to what is so functional and necessary – even if equally unlikely – that it makes conventional schemes, however likely they are to be repeated, seem insane by comparison.
