What are we posting about now? For the Right: immigration, Critical Race Theory, trans athletes, etc.. The next Total Emergency Issue -for both sides- will be AI. 
2028 election cycle: The grotesque anti-tech Left/ anti-tech Right will compete with each other for who can come up with most extreme and stupid crackdown scheme. 
Meet the Future Shock Coalition.
