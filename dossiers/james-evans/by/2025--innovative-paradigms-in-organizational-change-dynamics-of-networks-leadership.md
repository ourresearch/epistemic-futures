# Innovative Paradigms in Organizational Change: Dynamics of Networks, Leadership, and Creativity

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

This symposium explores how transformative periods in organizations create unique opportunities to advance management theory and practice. By integrating computational methods, interdisciplinary approaches, and empirical rigor, the session highlights how contemporary challenges can be addressed effectively. Four studies exemplify these advancements. They investigate diverse themes, including the impact of collaborative intensity on creativity, the influence of institutional logics on investment strategies, the reshaping of social networks by incentive pay programs, and the role of leadership adaptability in fostering innovation. Together, these papers showcase how computational and interdisciplinary research can address pressing organizational issues while offering actionable insights. Designed to resonate across divisions of the Academy of Management, the symposium bridges organizational behavior, development and change, and human resources, advancing our understanding of leadership, collaboration, and innovation. Through presentations and interactive discussions, it fosters dialogue on the integration of computational techniques and interdisciplinary strategies in managing organizational transformation and innovation. This session provides pathways for future research and practical applications, appealing to scholars, practitioners, and students alike.
