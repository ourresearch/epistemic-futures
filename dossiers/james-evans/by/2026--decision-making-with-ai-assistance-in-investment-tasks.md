# Decision-Making with AI Assistance in Investment Tasks

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

This study examines whether stance-biased AI investment coaches improve short-horizon portfolio decisions. Participants allocate a hypothetical $100,000 across a fixed universe of 25 financial assets for a 14-day paper-trading horizon. They first construct a baseline portfolio without AI assistance and are then randomly assigned to interact with one or two AI coaches whose recommendation styles vary in risk bias (e.g., risk-seeking, risk-averse, or neutral). After reviewing the AI’s evaluation and suggested changes, participants may revise their portfolio. The study measures whether risk-biased AI assistance improves portfolio decision quality relative to participants’ initial allocations.
