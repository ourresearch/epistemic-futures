## Quick bio

James Allen Evans is the Max Palevsky Professor of Sociology at the University of Chicago, where he directs [Knowledge Lab](https://knowledgelab.org/), is Faculty Director of the Masters Program in Computational Social Science, and is Faculty Co-Director of the Chicago Center for Computational Social Science; he is also External Professor at the Santa Fe Institute (verified against his [UChicago Sociology faculty page](https://sociology.uchicago.edu/directory/james-evans) and his [SFI profile](https://www.santafe.edu/people/profile/james-evans), 2026; B.A. Brigham Young 1994, M.A. Stanford 1999, Ph.D. Stanford 2004; ORCID [0000-0001-9838-0707](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9838-0707)). His research uses large-scale text and bibliographic data, machine learning and generative models to study how collectives think and know — the "science of science" / metaknowledge program, the design of experiments and hypotheses that accelerate discovery, and how AI reshapes what science asks and finds. Recurring findings across his work include that electronic publication and large teams narrow rather than broaden inquiry, that surprising or "distant" combinations drive impact, and that current AI tools expand individual scientists' output while contracting science's collective focus. He co-edits the *Journal of Social Computing* and *ACM Transactions on Social Computing*.

### Disambiguation (read this first)

"James Evans" is an extremely common author name and OpenAlex author `A5076633756` (**James A. Evans**, ORCID `0000-0001-9838-0707`, the correct anchor — ORCID's employment record reads "University of Chicago, Professor, from 2004-07") is a **contaminated composite**. Its 336 works mix in at least eight other James Evanses: a Barrett's-oesophagus/gastroenterology researcher (Queen Mary University of London / East & North Herts NHS), a US Army Corps of Engineers guided-wave/corrosion engineer, a British Geological Survey isotope archaeologist, a UC Berkeley metallurgist/battery researcher, a Manchester urban geographer, a UNC clinical-genomics researcher (James P. Evans), a SUNY Upstate milk-donation researcher, and a UWA finite-group-theory mathematician. Most contamination is pre-2005 and so falls outside the corpus window; **57 post-2005 works were manually excluded** by inspecting affiliation, co-author set, venue and topic.

Method used, and its confidence:
1. Resolved the author via ORCID (`0000-0001-9838-0707` → "James Allen Evans", University of Chicago) rather than by name search.
2. Pulled all 336 works on `A5076633756`, restricted to ≥2005 (265), and hand-classified every one against UChicago/Santa Fe Institute affiliation, the Knowledge Lab co-author set (Foster, Rzhetsky, Wu, Shi, Duede, Teplitskiy, Kozlowski, Xu, Kang, Bao, Aceves, Sengupta, Sourati, Kunievsky, Lai, Kim …) and topic mix (scientometrics, computational social science, NLP, sociology of science).
3. Verified a sample individually via DOI/publisher metadata.
4. Ran `raw_author_name.search` sweeps (`"James A. Evans"`, `"James Evans"`, `"Evans, James"`, `"Evans, James A."`, `"Evans, James Allen"`) scoped to UChicago and to Santa Fe Institute, plus `author.id` sweeps on 30+ frequent co-authors.
