# Second (Third?) Tumblr Linklog Attempt

## Full text

[I’m trying that thing again](<http://hapgood.tumblr.com/>) where I post a few Downes-style1 takes on random education articles I come across to Tumblr. This will likely be followed by that thing where I stop posting Downes-style takes on random articles. But the sad twitter-centric state of the artist formerly know as the edublogosphere has compelled me to try once again. I really shudder to think what would happen to the space if Downes stopped the [OLDaily](<http://www.downes.ca/news/OLDaily.htm>) project, especially now that people have fallen out of Google Readerdom.

For those unfamiliar with linklogs, click on the title to read the original article, and realize I write the summary in the space of about three to five minutes, so don’t expect the Critique of Pure Reason.

Anyway, go [check it out](<http://hapgood.tumblr.com/>). Subscribe to [the RSS](<http://hapgood.tumblr.com/rss>). And if you find it useful, let me know, it might help me figure out whether to slog through [the dip](<http://www.amazon.com/The-Dip-Little-Teaches-Stick/dp/1591841666>).

—–

1Yes, Downes-_style_. Quality and consistency, I don’t kid myself.
