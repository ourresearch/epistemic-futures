# Deadhead Sponsors Bill Creating Copyright Czar, Bush Signs

## Full text

Jim Groom [notes the creation of a Copyright Czar](<http://bavatuesdays.com/finally-us-has-a-copyright-czar/>). The bill is [here](<http://thomas.loc.gov/cgi-bin/bdquery/z?d110:SN03325:@@@L&summ2=m&>). I’m guessing since the “Drug Czar” idea worked out so well, the government just couldn’t help itself.

You want to know how screwed up the world is? The sponsor of this bill, Patrick Leahy, is a huge Grateful Dead fan. From [Wikipedia](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Leahy>):

> Leahy is a fan of the [Grateful Dead](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grateful_Dead> "Grateful Dead"). He has not only attended concerts, but has taped them, and
> 
> [](<http://commons.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:Patrick_Leahy_official_photo.jpg>)Image via Wikipedia
> 
> has a collection of the band’s tapes in his Senate Offices. [Jerry Garcia](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Garcia> "Jerry Garcia") visited him at his Senate offices, and Leahy gave a tie designed by Garcia to Senator [Orrin Hatch](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orrin_Hatch> "Orrin Hatch") (who responded by giving Leahy a [Rush Limbaugh](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rush_Limbaugh> "Rush Limbaugh") tie). Surviving band members [Bob Weir](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Weir> "Bob Weir") and [Mickey Hart](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Hart> "Mickey Hart") have participated in fundraisers for Leahy and his [Political Action Committee](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_Action_Committee> "Political Action Committee"), the Green Mountain Victory Fund. Leahy also appeared in a videotaped tribute to the Dead when they received a lifetime achievement award at the 2002 [Jammys](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammys> "Jammys"). His Senate website notes this response to a question from seventh grade students from Vermont’s [Thetford Academy](<http://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Thetford_Academy&action=edit&redlink=1> "Thetford Academy \(page does not exist\)") who asked Leahy which Dead song was his favorite, he replied: “… my favorite is “Black Muddy River” but we always play “[Truckin’](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truckin%27> "Truckin'")” on election night at my headquarters.”

Peace, Love, and Selling Out.
