# Peer Instruction Failure

## Full text

[Peer Instruction Failure](<http://advan.physiology.org/content/35/3/280.abstract>)

I’ve been making sure to note places where experimentation shows at least partial failure of Peer Instruction. This article (“[Effect of personal response systems on student perception and academic performance in courses in a health sciences curriculum](<http://advan.physiology.org/content/35/3/280.abstract>)”) is a good example of that. Only one of the sections fails, but it’s worth noting…..
