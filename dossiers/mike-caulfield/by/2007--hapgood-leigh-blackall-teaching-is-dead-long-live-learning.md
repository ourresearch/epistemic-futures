# Leigh Blackall: Teaching is Dead, Long Live Learning

## Full text

So [Leigh Blackall](<http://learnonline.wordpress.com/>) is my new favorite edublogger (Sorry Jim!).

If you want to know why, you can listen to [this podcast](<http://media.educationau.edu.au/gs06-day1-pm-blackall.mp3>).

Favorite thinker? Not sure. Thinker? It’s odd, but I feel these observations are just so obvious. I’m not sure I ever had to think them up, or that Leigh had to think them up, or that [Jim](<http://bavatuesdays.com>) had to think them up, or that [Jon](<http://blog.jonudell.net>) had to think them up or that even [Roger](<http://www.edge.org/3rd_culture/bios/schank.html>) had to think them up.

So it’s really unfair, but I don’t think of this stuff as shockingly brilliant. What I’m shocked by most often is why it’s not just obvious.

I mean, I put all this Web 2.0-speak on top of my explanations, but what I want to say most often to people is — so have you ever tried to accomplish a real world goal? Yeah? Well, it’s like that.
