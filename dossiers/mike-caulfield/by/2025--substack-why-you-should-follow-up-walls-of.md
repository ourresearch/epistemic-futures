# Why You Should "Follow Up": Walls of Benin

*An example of how an evidence-focused follow-up can make LLMs much smarter*

## Full text

One question comes up a bit, which is “If you got the answer you wanted the first round with an LLM why do you need to use a ‘follow-up’ at all?” 

The answer is simple. By telling the LLM to spend more effort on a smaller issue you direct its energy, just like a narrower search query can give you more detail on a subject. And by using an “evidence-focused” follow-up you have it attend to the elements of evidence and sourcing that are meaningful to your goals.

The video today was going to be about something else but it went in an interesting direction and gave me a chance to show how the “non-deterministic” output of LLMs makes follow-ups important, and why the combined strategy of “Get It In” (start broad) and “Follow-up” (drill into evidence or sourcing on a specific issue) gets you the best of both worlds.
