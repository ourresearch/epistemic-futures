# Front Page HuffPo

## Full text

So after all the political stories I’ve labored over, I finally made front page on [Huffington Post](<http://www.huffingtonpost.com>) — with a story I wrote in 5 minutes while downing my morning coffee.

[](<http://mikecaulfield.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/huffpo.gif> "huffpo.gif")

Oh well, I’ll take it! Thanks to the [Off The Bus](<http://www.huffingtonpost.com/off-the-bus>) crowd who have been pushing hard to get these stories by amateurs like me better placement.
