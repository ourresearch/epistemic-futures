# Occupy Wall Street protesters irked by rising student debt in U.S.

## Full text

[Occupy Wall Street protesters irked by rising student debt in U.S.](<http://marketplace.publicradio.org/display/web/2011/10/03/am-occupy-wall-street-focuses-on-rising-student-debt-in-us/>)

Worth remembering: they may be occupying Wall Street, but for many younger people in the crowd their main issue with the financial sector is student debt, and by extension, cost of college.

Maybe someone should do something about that?
