# Some Principles for AI-Assisted Search

*Slides from a thing*

## Full text

I did a thing today and made some slides. It was a short thing and there’s only a few. They are [here](<https://checkplease.neocities.org/why-sift-toolbox.pdf>). 

Lots of people know more about search than I do, I realize that. But after studying sense-making behavior of students during search for a decade, I do think I bring a wealth of experience understanding what good searchers know that poor searchers don’t, and my question lately is whether AI can help model some of the behaviors and understandings of good searchers — without taking agency from the novice.

And that’s what SIFT Toolbox is about, but my point with the slides is that SIFT Toolbox is about a larger set of ideas that could be adopted more generally, that make sure that we don’t lose the benefits of search in the quest for the perfect answer machine.
