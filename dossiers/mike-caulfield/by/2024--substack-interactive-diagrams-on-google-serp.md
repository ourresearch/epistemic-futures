# Interactive Diagrams on Google SERP

*Short note about a feature I hadn't seen before*

## Full text

I hadn’t seen this feature before on the SERP. This is in the [results for arsenic](<https://www.google.com/search?q=arsenic>):

[](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Vo3H!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcd3fe332-bd2f-457d-bd06-b1b998012a42_902x635.png>)

You can rotate it, etc. and it is also [directly linkable](<https://g.co/kgs/ozHup5R>). As is often the case with Google SERP features, it is somewhat difficult to find when it was introduced. It could be a while ago, I’m not in the habit of searching arsenic (a search I was exploring out of interest in the [recent](<https://www.npr.org/2024/07/11/nx-s1-5036484/tampons-heavy-metals-study>) and [seemingly overblown](<https://vajenda.substack.com/p/dont-panic-about-lead-in-tampons>) research on tampons, not as part of some Agatha Christie plot).

The arsenic visualization seems to be related to the [Signal Garden atom visualizer](<https://stem.signalgarden.com/>) on Google Play. Other result have different sources and formats. The visualization for salt is a [grab bag of diagrams](<https://g.co/kgs/1F91HFW>) where the interactivity is primarily labels and links. It looks like Google is mapping links to Google searches on top of the diagrams dynamically, though I can’t tell that for sure.

Right now I can only find the diagrams for atoms and molecules — if you’ve seen this panel in other searches let me know below.
