# Support Music over Lawyers. Buy Radiohead.

## Full text

The end is near. And that’s a very very good thing.

Radiohead is offering it’s newest album [on it’s website](<http://www.inrainbows.com/Store/Quickindex2.html>) for advance download.

The revolutionary thing? You pay what you want for it. Two dollars, ten dollars. Whatever. You make the call.

The experiment has even got [mainstream investing sites](<http://www.fool.com/investing/general/2007/10/02/radioheads-sonic-boom.aspx>) abuzz, saying that if this works, it could be the end of the pricefixing era of music.

Sometimes though, history needs a push. I’m going over to get the new album right after I finish posting this.

You should come with.

You should [go do it](<http://www.inrainbows.com/Store/Quickindex2.html>).
