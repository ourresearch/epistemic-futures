# One Minute Federated Wiki: Link Resolution From Journal History

## Full text

Link resolution in Smallest Federated Wiki can be confusing — depending on the page you are linked from links can go different places. 

This video shows simple link resolution from journal history. A second video showing slightly more complex scenarios where pages not in the journal are suggested as alternatives is forthcoming.
