## Small Addendum

One thing people sometimes say is “Look, that took 8 minutes, you can’t expect people to do that.” And to be clear, for an Easter Bunny meme I don’t! Note in this case if you had stopped at the first result — literally 30 seconds in — you would have gotten the right answer. And assuming you had some initial doubts, I imagine by that time the ridiculousness of the claim was sinking in. Thirty seconds in it is clearly fake!

The reply I often hear to that is, well, yeah, but because you didn’t trust the AI you had to check it and that took more time. You had to take more time to be absolutely sure!

Ok, well — you _always_ have to take more time if you want to be _absolutely_ sure. This is why one of the most important things to do when approaching any problem is to ask yourself how sure you need to be, and apply your effort accordingly. Most times the answer is “a bit more” but in some important instances the answer is “a lot”. 

That was true before AI, it was true for search, it was true a million years ago as our ancestors walked the African plains. 

If it seems too much work to be that level of certain, stop with the first response, just as you might stop at the first result page in a web search. The answer to the question of “How will I know when to stop” is always going to be tied to “How sure do I want to be?” and that is not something new in the least. You can ask that question and you should, but the answer is ultimately up to you.
