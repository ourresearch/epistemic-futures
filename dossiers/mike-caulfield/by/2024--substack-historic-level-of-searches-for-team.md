# Trend of the Day: Historic level of searches for Team USA shirts

*It's a just a pattern I noticed, it's got a small search volume -- but it's definitely got me wondering if there's a vibe shift underway.*

## Full text

I’ve been feeling like there is a new patriotism emerging lately, partially in reaction to the weird anti-Olympics fervor by some in the U.S. — and maybe more just part of a more general vibe shift. My experience is an _n_ of one of course, but this year is probably the first time I’ve thought of buying a U.S. Olympics shirt since I was a kid in 1984. So I got interested in whether others seemed to be feeling the same way. And the answer might be yes? The search trend is interesting, to say the least.

[](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mkhz!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4bd5a709-af23-4039-b16b-93da58d2fbe7_1711x603.png>)Searches for Team USA shirts over past 20 years. Recent searches aren’t finalized, but could be double that of any previous Olympics cycle.

[](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!OSH-!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ffba237c8-2c12-4150-8eda-cb19f97067c9_1710x527.png>)The same graph over past 5 years, also showing a doubling over the 2021 Olympics.

**These are of course pretty small search volumes** , and the history mixes in other Team USA events such as soccer championships and the like. So caveat, YMMV, etc. But it’s definitely catching my interest as a pattern. I don’t know if this means anything, but I’ll try and think up some related views over the next few days and push on it a bit.
