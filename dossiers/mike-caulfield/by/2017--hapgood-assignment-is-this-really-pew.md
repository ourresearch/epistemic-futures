# Assignment: Is This Really Pew?

## Full text

The Pew Research Center is a good source of information on many issues; much of our current public debate is informed by data they collect.

So here’s a question: is the site at [people-press.org](<http://www.people-press.org/2017/01/12/the-world-facing-trump-public-sees-isis-cyberattacks-north-korea-as-top-threats/>) really Pew? Or is it an imposter? How do you know?
