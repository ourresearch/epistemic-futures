# A blog for those teaching and learning web literacy

## Full text

So this will be a blog where I take various examples of things one finds on the internet (or in their email) and talk through how I would use them to teach web literacy to students. It’s intended for those teaching web literacy for students, to give people examples to use in class. But I think even those who study misinformation may learn a thing or two from it.  
  
The term “taking bearings” comes from the online literacy work of Sam Wineburg and Sarah McGrew.
