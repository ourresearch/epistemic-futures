# Three-Car Pileup on the Fake Photo Highway

*AI slop is bad, but pollution of the historical record is an internet tradition*

## Full text

[](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7lWe!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdf7ae850-babd-4819-8a89-cafe6f9b97da_1864x1119.png>)

Chalk this one up to "The more things change..." 

An AI-generated "photo" of "female samurai" Nakano Takeko (a real historical figure) goes viral on Facebook. 

Commenter complains and suggests that instead of AI the poster use one of the "real" photos of Takeko. Both photos suggested are historical photos, but of Japanese actresses years later recreating the imagined look of a female warrior, and have circulated wrongly described as being Tekeko for a long time on the internet. 

There is no known photograph of Nakano Takeko.
