# Medical Student Debt is a Significant Subpopulation of Student Debt

## Full text

From [here](<http://www.usatoday.com/money/perfi/college/story/2011-10-19/student-loan-debt/50818676/1>):

> **The amount of student loans taken out last year crossed the $100 billion mark** for the first time and total loans outstanding will exceed $1 trillion for the first time this year. Americans now owe more on student loans than on credit cards, reports the [Federal Reserve Bank of New York](<http://content.usatoday.com/topics/topic/Organizations/Government+Bodies/Federal+Reserve+Bank+of+New+York> "More news, photos about Federal Reserve Bank of New York"), the U.S. Department of Education and private sources.

From [here](<http://well.blogs.nytimes.com/2011/07/28/the-hidden-costs-of-medical-student-debt/>):

> For almost three generations, debt has been a nearly inescapable part of becoming a doctor. Over 80 percent of each medical student class will graduate in debt; and while that percentage has remained unchanged for 25 years, the increase in the total amount owed has leapfrogged over all other economic [reality checks, like inflation and the consumer price index.](<http://content.healthaffairs.org/content/24/2/527.full.html>)According to the Association of American Medical Colleges, which has been trying to address the problem for nearly a decade, **young doctors who graduated from medical school last year had an[average debt of $158,000](<https://www.aamc.org/download/152968/data/10debtfactcard.pdf>), or[$2.3 billion for the group as a whole](<http://journals.lww.com/academicmedicine/Abstract/2011/07000/A_History_of_Medical_Student_Debt__Observations.16.aspx>).** Almost a third of students owed more than $200,000, a number that will only increase with the addition of interest over payback periods of 25 to 30 years.

There’s a per year/per education problem with comparing any numbers here. But the scale is such that medical student debt probably accounts for a nontrivial amount of the difference between [mean and median student debt](<http://hapgood.us/2012/03/07/mean-vs-median-student-loan-debt/> "Mean vs. Median Student Loan Debt"). [Law school debt](<http://www.nytimes.com/2011/01/09/business/09law.html?_r=1&adxnnl=1&pagewanted=all&adxnnlx=1331129427-dy5/H1zoaOJr4jXMeX2yAw>) is may be another factor, though I don’t have any good numbers on that yet.
