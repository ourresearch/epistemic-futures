# Video & audio — Mike Caulfield

Noted, not transcribed (per CONVENTIONS.md). Where a show published a text transcript,
that transcript is saved in `by/` and is flagged below.

**Identity warning for anyone extending this list:** there are at least two other
public "Mike Caulfield"s. A **Formula 1 race strategist** named Mike Caulfield appears on
many podcasts (Missed Apex F1, DNF1, Cut To The Race, The Silver Arrows Podcast) — not him.
**Timothy Caulfield** (health-law scholar, University of Alberta) also works on
misinformation and is a genuine confusion risk. Verify affiliation (UW Center for an
Informed Public / WSU Vancouver) before adding anything.
