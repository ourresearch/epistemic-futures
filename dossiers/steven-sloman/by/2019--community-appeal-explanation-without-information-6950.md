# Community Appeal: Explanation without Information

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

Formal or categorical explanation involves the use of a label to explain a feature associated with an object or group of objects. We provide evidence that label entrenchment, the degree to which a label is accepted and used by members of the community, influences the judged quality of a categorical explanation whether or not the explanation offers substantive information about the explanandum. This repository includes all data gathered for the experiments, as well as texts of scenarios used.
