# Artifact Categorization: The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

Abstract A sizeable subfield of cognitive psychology is devoted to how humans categorize entities in their world, yet there has been little explicit consideration of what it means to categorize. One straightforward perspective, the one perhaps held at an intuitive level by most laypeople, is that categories are objectively defined, metaphysically real groupings of entities.
