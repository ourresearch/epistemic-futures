# A problem we don't talk enough about

*How did men and boys fall behind in America? with Richard Reeves*

## Full text

So let’s talk about the new show (which you can see here on YouTube, and listen to on Apple Podcasts and Spotify):

This week, I talk with Richard Reeves about an issue where culture, money, and politics are inextricably linked: the state of boys and men.

Why have boys fallen behind in K-12 education, and higher ed? (This is true even of upper-income boys - though they are more insulated than lower-income boys.)

Why do women far outnumber men in higher education?

What has happened that has led millions of prime-age men to leave the labor force?

And how does politics - and the fortunes of the Democrats - factor into all of this?

There could be no one better to talk about this than Reeves. Every minute of our conversation fascinated me.

Reeves is the author of the bestselling book Of Men and Boys - which, as he told me, he couldn’t get anyone to publish initially.

The book tries to get to the bottom of the issues facing boys and men, why this is happening now, how it affects women, and - most importantly - what we can do.

How can schools help boys, and young men?

How can we offer more positive messages to teenagers?

And how do we help men feel a sense of belonging, in a world in which may aspects of belonging - from marriage to religious communities - are simply less common than they once were?

As always, I’d love to hear your thoughts, feedback, and questions.
