# The Biggest Loser

*MAHA has a point. And a problem.*

## Full text

Hi Friends,

Once upon a time, a physicist became fascinated by nutrition.

Ultimately, after a stint in biotech, he got a job as a researcher at the National Institutes of Health, where he spent more than twenty years rigorously testing the kinds of claims that diet gurus make with certainty:

High-protein diets make you eat less!

Ultra processed foods make you eat more!

Kevin Hall also had a number of unique opportunities, including studying contestants on the reality show The Biggest Loser - and following up with them six years later. What happened to the contestants over time? Who regained weight, and who didn’t?

On this week’s show, Hall - a scientist who has never approached nutrition or metabolism with a particular agenda - talks about why many of our assumptions about weight loss are wrong. And he explains what we still don’t know.

[Listen to the show: YouTube, Apple Podcasts, Spotify]

Plus, we talk about why Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy, Jr. has - in many ways - correctly diagnosed some of the problems in our food system. But Hall also details why the minor changes Kennedy has so far enacted are unlikely to actually make us much healthier.

What would Kennedy have to do to improve American diets? Hall has thoughts.
