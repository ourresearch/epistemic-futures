# Rethinking The Way We Work

## Full text

How does your workplace operate? Do you and your coworkers have the freedom to try things and fail, or step back from the madness and simply brainstorm?

Jason Fried, co-founder and president of Chicago-based 37 Signals, says it’s time for a radical shift in the way we approach work. He joined Kara Miller to talk about a radical new vision.
