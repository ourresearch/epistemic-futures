# The Mobile Wave

## Full text

Mobile computing- whether from a smartphone or a tablet- is fundamentally changing our lives. It’s shifting the ways we learn, where we shop, how we work. Kara Miller speaks with author Michael Saylor on Boston Public Radio about what the new mobile world order as more people acquire devices like laptops, tablets, and smartphones.
