# The Future of Medicine

## Full text

I. What if you could awaken dormant heart cells, offering renewed vitality to those who have suffered heart attacks? Kara Miller speaks with Dr. Deepak Srivastava, a world leader in cardiovascular and stem cell research, about his exciting new findings.

II. Dr. Timothy Galitski and Dr. Tom Hawn have begun to examine medical treatments and drugs in a radically new way. Instead of focusing on the minute - diseased cells, for example - they suggest looking at the whole person, which might fundamentally change how medicine is practiced.

III. Kara Miller speaks with entrepreneurs trying to bring game-changing innovation to doctors’ offices and hospital rooms. Welcome to Healthbox, the brainchild of Nina Nashif.
