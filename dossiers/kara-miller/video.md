## Innovation Hub (GBH/WGBH → PRI → PRX), 2011–2021 — she created, hosted and was executive editor

**678 episodes and segments are enumerated in [`innovation-hub-episode-index.tsv`](innovation-hub-episode-index.tsv)** (date, title, duration, audio URL, tags, description), covering 2016-10-07 to 2021-10-08 — the full run of the
public feed `https://feeds.wgbh.org/106/feed-rss.xml`. Episodes are roughly 50 minutes for full
shows and 8–25 minutes for single segments; the show is an interview program, so each item is
one or more long-form interviews conducted by Miller.

Show feed: https://feeds.wgbh.org/106/feed-rss.xml · Program page: https://theworld.org/programs/innovation-hub

The feed reaches back only to October 2016. **Episodes from the 2011–2016 half of the run are
not in the feed**; their existence is attested by the GBH web write-ups in `by/` (2012–2013) and
the theworld.org program archive (`theworld-innovationhub-post-index.tsv`, 535 posts back to
2012-07-20). Recovering the pre-2016 audio list would need a Wayback pass over the old feed.
