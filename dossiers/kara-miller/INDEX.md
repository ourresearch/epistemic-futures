## Quick bio

Kara Miller is a Boston journalist and broadcaster. She is a Correspondent at **The Boston
Globe**, where since late 2021 she has written **"The Big Idea,"** a business/ideas column, and
contributes long features to the Globe Magazine. She created and hosted **"Innovation Hub"**
(GBH/WGBH, 2011–2021), a weekly hour-long public-radio interview program about technology,
education, science and business; PRI took it national in 2014 and it aired on more than 100
stations, winning the 2021 Clarion Award for best Radio Talk or Interview Program. Since October
2025 she has hosted **"It Turns Out,"** an independent interview podcast and YouTube show about new
research, produced through her company **Laurel Way Productions** and supported by the Alfred P.
Sloan Foundation (Grant No. G-2025-79141); it has a free companion Substack newsletter. She holds a
B.A. from Yale and a Ph.D. in English from Tufts (2008), and has taught at Babson College and the
University of Massachusetts. She sits on the advisory committee of The Lemelson Foundation (since
2016) and of *LearningWell* magazine (since 2023).

### Conventions ambiguities hit on this pass

1. **A show write-up bylined to the host is not a transcript, but it is her byline.** The 144 GBH and
   24 theworld.org items are *web articles about her own radio segments*, bylined to her (often with
   a producer), that summarize the interview and quote the guest rather than transcribing it. They go
   in `by/` on the affirmative-byline rule, but the `content:` field can't distinguish "her prose" from
   "her prose quoting her guest at length." Every one of these files says so in `notes:`. **A rule for
   how much of the corpus may be a person's paraphrase of someone else's speech would help** — this is
   the same risk the multi-guest-transcript ruling addresses, in a different shape.
2. **Unbylined institutional write-ups of the person's own show.** 286 theworld.org posts are
   adaptations of *her* segments with no byline at all. The "sole-public-author organisation"
   carve-out does not apply (the show had a staff of producers), so they were excluded from `by/` and
   recorded in a sidecar TSV. Worth codifying explicitly: **a show is not an author.**
4. **`date:` for an undated institutional bio page.** The Lemelson and GBH bios carry no publication
   date. Retrieval date was used for one and the last relevant event date for the other, with the
   choice stated in `notes:`.
