## Quick bio

Tui Shaub (publishes and appears in staff listings variously as **Tui Shaub**, **Tui Cord**, and **Tui Shaub Cord**) is an editor and philanthropy strategist working on the societal effects of generative AI. Most recently at **Omidyar Network** she was Principal, Strategic Engagement and Research, in the Office of the CEO — a role her staff page described as exploring "the second and third order effects of technologies like generative AI on social systems and epistemic infrastructures" and leading "sense-making efforts" ([Omidyar Network staff page, Wayback 2024-12-14](https://web.archive.org/web/20241214003129/https://omidyar.com/omidyar_team/tui-shaub/)). Before that she was **Associate Director of the Transformations of the Human (ToftH) program at the Berggruen Institute** and an **Associate Editor of Noema Magazine** ([Berggruen people page](https://berggruen.org/people/tui-shaub); [Noema Issue I masthead, June 2020](https://www.noemamag.com/introducing-noema-issue-i-the-great-acceleration)), and earlier Articles Editor at *World Affairs Journal*, Chief of Staff at the Y&S Nazarian Family Foundation, a co-founder of the charity Future Brilliance US, and a marketing/research lead for the Mayor of London's affordable-housing work and the Royal Institute of British Architects. She holds an MA in US Studies (History and Politics) from University College London and a BA in Politics, Philosophy and History from Birkbeck College, University of London.
