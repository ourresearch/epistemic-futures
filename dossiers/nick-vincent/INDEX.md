## Quick bio

Nicholas ("Nick") M. Vincent is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing Science at
Simon Fraser University (since 2023), where he directs the People- and Data-centric Computing
research group (padcomp.org). He is affiliated with SFU's Digital Democracies Institute and
School of Public Policy, and is Research Director at Metagov. He received his PhD in 2022 from
Northwestern University's Technology and Social Behavior program (a joint CS/Communication
degree) under Brent Hecht, with a dissertation on "data leverage" that won the 2024 ACM SIGCHI
Outstanding Dissertation Award; he was then a postdoc with the Computational Communication
Research Lab at UC Davis and the Social Futures Lab at the University of Washington. His research
studies the content ecosystems and data supply chains behind search engines, recommender systems,
and generative AI — data strikes and conscious data contribution, data dividends, Wikipedia's value
to search engines and language models, licensing and consent for training data, and "Public AI."
He publishes at ACM CHI/CSCW/FAccT, AAAI ICWSM, NeurIPS and AIES, and writes the Data Leverage
newsletter/digital garden. (Verified against https://www.nickmvincent.com/ and
https://www.nickmvincent.com/cv/, last updated 2026-06-15, retrieved 2026-08-13.)
