## Notes on topic coverage

The recorded material clusters into four groups:

1. **Weaponized interdependence / economic statecraft** — the largest group: his own two
   self-uploaded lecture versions (2019), the CSIS, Lawfare, Foreign Affairs, Long Now,
   Sanctions Age and Eurelectric appearances, and the *Underground Empire* book-tour talks
   with Abraham Newman.
2. **AI as a social/cultural technology** — the 2025-26 lecture circuit built on the *Science*
   paper with Gopnik, Shalizi and Evans: the Blavatnik School Astor Lecture (Oxford), the
   Watson Institute lecture (Brown), and the Mercatus conversation.
3. **Democracy, information and collective problem-solving** — Mindscape 148, the Cornell CSES
   session with Margaret Levi on RCTs and democratic experimentalism, the Plurality Institute
   clip, and the Agile Rabbit "Tech vs Trust" event with Glen Weyl.
4. **Bloggingheads.tv / Nonzero archive (c. 2008-2012, re-uploaded to YouTube)** — roughly a
   dozen long diavlogs with Daniel Drezner, Robert Wright, Ross Douthat, Will Wilkinson,
   Brink Lindsey, Cass Sunstein, Robert Farley and Jennifer Martinez. Wikipedia's External
   Links section points at this same Bloggingheads corpus. These are the densest surviving
   record of his 2000s-era voice outside Crooked Timber and have no transcripts.
