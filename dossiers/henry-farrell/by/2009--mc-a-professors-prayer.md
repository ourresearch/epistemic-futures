# A professor’s prayer

## Full text

From [Thoreau](http://highclearing.com/index.php/archives/2009/06/07/9397) at _Unqualified Offerings._

bq. Lord, grant me the courage to fail the students I cannot pass, the strength to grade 100+ finals and 20+ lab reports, and the fortitude to withstand the consequences if a mentally unstable grade grubber gets an A-.

Replace ‘lab reports’ with ‘final papers’ and it travels nicely.
