# If only politics were everywhere: a call for political action

## Full text

The Monkey Cage does not endorse political parties, programs, candidates etc. I nonetheless suspect that [John Scalzi’s proposal](http://whatever.scalzi.com/2010/03/08/a-quick-note-to-universal-home-entertainment/) is one that everyone could get behind.

bq. I’m generally not one for overbearing government regulation in entertainment, but if someone were to introduce legislation requiring home entertainment companies to have a “just play the damn movie” button at the start of every DVD, Blu-Ray or any other future movie-playing technology, I would call my Senators and representative every fifteen minutes until they voted “yes” on that bill. That is all.
