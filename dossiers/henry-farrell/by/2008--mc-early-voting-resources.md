# Early voting resources

## Full text

Many of the readers of the Monkey Cage are probably already aware of these resources, but for those who aren’t, the best I am aware of are Michael McDonald’s [site](http://elections.gmu.edu/early_vote_2008.html) (if you like a frequently updated table), and the Early Voting Information Center’s [blog](http://www.earlyvoting.net/blog/) (if you like chatty discussion and graphs). I’m aware that McDonald is on one side of a debate about how best to measure turnout, but since this isn’t my area of political science, I’m in no position to pontificate on the merits of the one or the other approach …
