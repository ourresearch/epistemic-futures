# Data Visualizations in Everything: Plagiarizing Politicians Edition

## Full text

From [Vis4.net](http://vis4.net/blog/en/posts/guttenberg-plagiarism/,) a visualization of the plagiarized bits in the Ph.D. dissertation of Karl-Theodor zu Guttenberg, defense minister of Germany until a couple of days ago. Dark red bars show full-on plagiarism, bright red various other categories of dodginess, and gray bars show apparently original text. Via [FlowingData](http://flowingdata.com/2011/03/02/german-defense-ministers-plagiarized-phd-dissertation-visualized/.)
