# Vanessa Williamson Guestblogging

## Full text

Over the next several days, [Vanessa Williamson](http://www.gov.harvard.edu/graduate-program/graduate-student-contact-list/vanessa-williamson) will be guestblogging. She and Theda Skocpol have co-authored “The Tea Party and The Remaking of American Conservatism” ([Powells](http://www.powells.com/partner/29956/biblio/9780199832637?p_wgt), [Amazon](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0199832633/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=henryfarrell-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=0199832633)). We covered some of their research (with John Coggin) on The Monkey Cage [here](http://tmc.org/blog/2011/05/12/fox-news-as-a-social-movement/).  We are very happy to have her with us.
