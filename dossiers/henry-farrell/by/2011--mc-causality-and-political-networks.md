# Causality and political networks

## Full text

Michael Heaney has put together an interesting [article/symposium of essays](http://www-personal.umich.edu/~mheaney/Causality_in_Political_Networks.pdf) on the topic. I would be interested to see what [Cosma Shalizi](http://arxiv.org/abs/1004.4704) has to say about e.g. Betsy Sinclair’s and James Fowler’s contributions on separating out causal effects from homophily. Heaney’s own contribution – on the problems of inferring network structure from survey data – is very useful.
