# Pairwise Ranking Ownzored

## Full text

It looks as though the [pairwise ranking system](http://www.allourideas.org/poliscirankings/results) discussed a few days ago has been corrupted by automated clicking scripts. More [here](http://www.poliscijobrumors.com/topic.php?id=32548) (including a couple of comments from Kieran). As far as I know, the same is not true of similar ranking systems generated for sociology and philosophy departments. Whether this says good or bad things about political science grad students as compared to grad students in other disciplines, I leave to you all to decide for yourselves …
