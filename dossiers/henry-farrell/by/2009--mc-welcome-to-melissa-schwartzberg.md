# Welcome to Melissa Schwartzberg

## Full text

Over the next couple of weeks, we will have our firstsecond (I forgot Jennifer Hochschild’s guest-stint to my shame)  political theorist guest blogging with us: [Melissa Schwartzberg](http://www.columbia.edu/~ms3125/). Melissa is an associate professor in the department of political science at Columbia University.  She is also a former colleague of John, Lee, David and myself at GWU, and a co-author of both Lee and myself (her [paper with Lee](http://journals.cambridge.org/action/displayAbstract;jsessionid=FB7E9D9E14A97B320F589639146CA171.tomcat1?fromPage=online&aid=428332) is a lot more entertaining than her [paper with me](http://www.cceia.org/resources/journal/22_4/essays/002.html)). She’s writing a new book on majority and unanimity rule. Welcome Melissa!
