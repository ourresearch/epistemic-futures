# Political Scientists elected to NAS

## Full text

Just in, (via a tweet from APSA) Larry Bartels and James Fearon have [been elected](http://www.nasonline.org/news-and-multimedia/news/2012_05_01_NAS_Election.html) to the National Academy of Sciences. Obviously, we have particular reason to be pleased – both are occasional contributors to the Monkey Cage (this correlation is emphatically not causation). Warm congratulations to both.
