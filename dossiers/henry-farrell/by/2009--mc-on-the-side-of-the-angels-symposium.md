# On the Side of the Angels symposium

## Full text

Jacob Levy has organized a [symposium](http://jacobtlevy.blogspot.com/search/label/Rosenblum-symposium) on Nancy Rosenblum’s new apologia for parties and partisanship, _On the Side of the Angels_ at his blog. Participants include Jacob, Nancy Rosenblum herself, Melissa Schwartzberg, Patrick Deneen, Nadia Urbinati, Mara Marin, and, well, me. This is a book which is of interest not only to political theorists, but political scientists too (Rosenblum engages with the political science literature far more extensively than the average theorist does).
