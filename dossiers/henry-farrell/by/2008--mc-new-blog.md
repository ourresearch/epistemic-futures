# New blog

## Full text

Via [Dan Nexon](http://duckofminerva.blogspot.com/2008/04/charles-tilly-video-interview.html,) I see that Daniel Little has a blog, [Understanding Society](http://understandingsociety.blogspot.com/) with lots of interesting stuff. Little’s book [Varieties of Social Explanation](http://www.amazon.com/gp/redirect.html?ie=UTF8&location=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.amazon.com%2Fgp%2Fproduct%2F0813305667&tag=henryfarrell-20&linkCode=ur2&camp=1789&creative=9325) is my favourite introduction to the philosophy of social science, and his blog is very good too. It’s part of a larger project, which includes [interviews](http://www.changingsociety.org/ChangingSociety/Interviews.html) with Charles Tilly, Sidney Tarrow and other pol-sci luminaries. Recommended.
