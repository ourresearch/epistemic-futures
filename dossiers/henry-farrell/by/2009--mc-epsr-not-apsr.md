# EPSR, not APSR

## Full text

Via [Francois at Polit’Bistro](http://politbistro.hypotheses.org/167,) the _European Political Science Review_ has just published its [first issue](http://journals.cambridge.org/action/displayJournal?jid=EPR.) Polit’Bistro’s discussion of the uncomfortable relationship between political science in Europe and America (the former seeking both to emulate and distinguish itself from the latter) provides helpful background. And is it just me that gets a collision-of-worlds feeling when I see a journal’s front page having links to Digg, Furl, Reddit (Aaron – feel proud!) etc?
