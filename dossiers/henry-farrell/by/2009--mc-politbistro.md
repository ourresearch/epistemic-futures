# Polit’bistro

## Full text

Via Arthur Goldhammer, a [new blog](https://politbistro.hypotheses.org/) (in French), on political science and France. I am aware of a couple of previous efforts to get something like this up and running, but this may be the one that succeeds. It is nicely put together, very well designed and with a good mixture of political science and current affairs commentary in the posts that have been published to date.

This post on “[Nicholas Sarkozy and presidential style](https://politbistro.hypotheses.org/107)” is a nice example, drawing on findings from both U.S. and French political science. Perhaps at some point they might like to take a go at Roger Cohen’s “[One France is Enough](https://www.nytimes.com/2009/03/05/opinion/05Cohen.html?ref=opinion)” (if they do, I promise to translate the major points for a U.S. audience.
