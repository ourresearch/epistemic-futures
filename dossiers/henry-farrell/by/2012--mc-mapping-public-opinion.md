# Mapping Public Opinion

## Full text

[David Sparks](http://dsparks.wordpress.com/2011/10/24/isarithmic-maps-of-public-opinion-data/) has some very nice maps of public opinion data. What’s impressive is how they combine data sparsity (which also gives us a rough and ready estimate of how the population is distributed) with information on respondents’ party ID. Via Cosma Shalizi.
