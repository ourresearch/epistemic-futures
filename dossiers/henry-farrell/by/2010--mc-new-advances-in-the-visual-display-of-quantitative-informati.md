# New advances in the visual display of quantitative information

## Full text

The BBC will [project a bar chart](http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/politics/election_2010/8656578.stm) of the number of seats won by the Conservatives, Labour, and Liberal Democrats on the evening of the electoral count, and update it in realtime.

![](http://newsimg.bbc.co.uk/media/images/47762000/jpg/_47762144_electionimage240.jpg)

In other UK election news, Brown political scientist and expatriate Scotsman, Mark Blyth has a [co-written](http://www.foreignaffairs.com/articles/66402/mark-blyth-and-jonathan-hopkin/labour-pains) piece on the long term consequences of the election on the _Foreign Affairs_  website today.
