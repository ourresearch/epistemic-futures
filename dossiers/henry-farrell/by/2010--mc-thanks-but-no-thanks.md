# Thanks. But no thanks.

## Full text

TPM Muckraker [talks to the proprietor](http://tpmmuckraker.talkingpointsmemo.com/2010/07/research_2000_president_has_two_degrees_in_recreation.php) of scandal-hit polling firm, Research 2000.

bq. “I consider myself a political scientist,” Ali told TPMmuckraker in an interview today. “If you want to call me a statistical wiz, I am not.” Ali’s academic history is primarily in recreation. He graduated from the University of Maryland with a BS in Recreation (Public Health) in 1983, according to a university spokesman. Records show he also got an MA in Recreation from Maryland in 1991.
