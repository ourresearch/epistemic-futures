# Duck of Minerva Blogging Awards

## Full text

The people at Duck of Minerva have asked me to publicize nominations for their [blogging awards](http://www.whiteoliphaunt.com/duckofminerva/2012/12/2012-international-studies-blogging-awards-final-call.html) in international studies. Today is the last day for nominations. Awards like this are best suited to publicizing blogs or blogposts that otherwise would receive less attention than they deserve, but feel free to nominate (or not) as you like.
