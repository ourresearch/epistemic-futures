# Pull your blog posts (with pageviews!) into ImpactStory

## Full text

\

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/IAE6xeS9TR_iU3_4AwXuG3kxd4I-JcbVo5oAtN1W1W-rMRw28chPHMVR_e_4rFiGYKCBX5rZnKmQYXvguXRl-cSmNUw-gW1nc0LnrwT1CcYY5tuhOdiVMUxwfQ)

Our blog is part of our scholarly identity, for many of us.  We discuss papers we’ve read, ponder issues we’ve been thinking about, and sometimes release our own early results. Wouldn’t it be great if we could showcase these mini publications on our CV, ideally linked to their readership and discussions in the blogosphere.

Good news: now it’s easy!  Starting today, your blog gets it’s own section on your ImpactStory profile. When you import a blog, we automatically find your most-tweeted posts and pull them into your profile, linked to their tweets, bookmarks, and other metrics. You can curate this list by adding and removing individual posts to feature the ones you’re most proud of.

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/W3aYA4Q0prkCnmaLOzQkP8oupTSJ3pm3r1GDS0Y4mON2XmAGYYGiT_vQezJ3LXAaLQv3R4BMQyomj0ts9GcT4zbOnQl0EB4V7GAtL9Wk2u4fG6El3XEAHtVtRw)

Even better, if your blog is hosted on WordPress.com you’ll see still more metrics — comments, subscribers, and even pageviews!

We’re excited about the way this rounds out the story you can tell about yourself on your product list, and we think you will be too.  Go give it a try (and if you haven’t done so yet, [pull in your top tweets](http://blog.impactstory.org/2013/12/12/tweets/), [hook up your figshare account](http://blog.impactstory.org/2013/12/02/link-your-figshare-and-impactstory-accounts/), and [add some videos](http://blog.impactstory.org/2013/11/04/videos/)!).

**Update:** Topsy has ended data access, meaning this feature is no longer available for Impactstory profiles. We’re looking into ways to restore it as soon as we can. If you’re interested in this feature, please [vote for it in our Feedback forum](http://feedback.impactstory.org/forums/166950-general/suggestions/5577251-let-me-import-my-blog).
