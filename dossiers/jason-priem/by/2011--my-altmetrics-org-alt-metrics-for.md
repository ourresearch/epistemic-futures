# my.altmetrics.org: alt-metrics for your CV

## Full text

The [PLoS alt-metrics study](https://github.com/jasonpriem/plos_altmetrics_study/wiki) is moving well; we’ve transitioned to an [open notebook](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Notebook_Science) built on [GitHub](https://github.com/) (which is awesome and a good topic for another post) and findings are starting to emerge.

I’m starting to think about next steps, and to me the obvious one is to build a frontend for our [crawler](https://github.com/jasonpriem/plos_altmetrics_study/tree/master/crawler)–giving working scholars and funders the opportunity to try out [alt-metrics](http://altmetrics.org/manifesto) for themselves.

I’m posting some rough mockups (click ‘em to [embiggen](http://linguistlist.org/issues/10/10-261.html)) of what a public alt-metrics machine might look like. This would be a place where people could upload a list of DOIs (or some other ID) and get a page that’ll let them track, visualise, and analyse the impact of their work in a broader and faster way than citations alone allow–sort of a Google Analytics for your CV.

I’m thinking it’d be cool to also have a way to embed results in another webpage, so you could make your actual CV show alt-metrics in real time. But there are a lot of directions to take this. If you’ve got suggestions, I’d love to hear!
