# Portrait of the artist as a phrenology illustration

## Full text

[![An assignment in my infoVis class: self-portrait as a phrenology illustration](http://jasonpriem.com/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/phrenology-interests-256x300.jpg "An assignment in my infoVis class: self-portrait as a phrenology illustration")](http://jasonpriem.com/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/phrenology-interests.jpg)The first assignment in my infoVis class was to make a visual introduction to ourselves.  I drew a self-portrait in profile, then added my categorized interests in the style of a 19th-century [phrenology](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phrenology) illustration (compare with actual period illustrations [here](http://www.as.ua.edu/history/new/html/faculty/rothman.html) and [here](http://mysite.verizon.net/res0im1v/donettesteelepsychology/)).

Phrenology is interesting stuff.  Though phrenologists had nearly everything wrong, modern neuroimaging has demonstrated that one  important part of their core idea was right: many psychological functions really are highly localized in the brain.  And they made a lot of really cool infographics.  Actually, maybe this is pseudoscience in general; palmistry and astrology also make silly data into some neat-looking infovis.  This same exercise would be fun with made-up star charts and palm diagrams.
