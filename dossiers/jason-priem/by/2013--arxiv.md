# you asked, you got it: support for arXiv IDs

## Full text

\

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/KO_uSiBnQi_4YeHY4xvHQg3PQjMcmIljjhmzKk03uQL82NLLDLpspO3VYlhQobp52ukSqhIKD2dwPYc4FJdi6rkl62aYuFYa1wxXpZU6B8I-ejNrS_GMamd1Aw)We love requests for new ImpactStory features.  The second-most requested feature at [feedback.impactstory.org](http://feedback.impactstory.org) has been support for arXiv IDs.  So, starting today, you’ve got’em!

You can import your preprints by arXiv id — we’ll retrieve the title and author list, then look up all the metrics we can find.  Interestingly, arXiv is [actively against sharing download stats](http://arxiv.org/help/faq/statfaq), but maybe with enough encouragement from you they’ll update their policy?

Thanks for everyone who took the time to vote on the feature, and keep those requests coming!
