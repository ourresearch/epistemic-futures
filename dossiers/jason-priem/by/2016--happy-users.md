# Why researchers are loving the new Impactstory

## Full text

\
We put our heart and soul into the new Impactstory and have been on pins and needles to hear what you think.  Well it’s been a week and the verdict is in — we’re hearing that the new version is [awesome](https://twitter.com/ReaderMeter/status/718868570369699848), [fantastic](https://twitter.com/CrispComm/status/721087712812515328), and [truly excellent](https://twitter.com/johnpoverington/status/718498927113330693), a [home run](https://twitter.com/andreaketchum/status/719727979178434561) and [must-have](https://twitter.com/Protohedgehog/status/718512521263607808)–an academic profile that’s [exciting and relevant](https://twitter.com/FarahAlShorbaji/status/719066183497084928).

And so much more. So much more, in fact, that we wanted to a little break from the frenzied responding, bugfixing, and feature-launching we’ve been doing this week and summarize a bit of what we’ve heard.

## What do you like?

A lot of users have appreciated that it now [takes seconds](https://twitter.com/karenanya/status/719454704233771008) and is [super easy](https://twitter.com/RemiDaigle/status/718511722634457088) to set up a profile that’s blazing fast and smooth to use: it’s [instant insights about your research](https://twitter.com/karenanya/status/719454704233771008).

Unlike speed, beauty is in the eye of the beholder–but our beholders seem delightfully agreed that our new look is [great](https://twitter.com/ftmaestre/status/718703204813447168), [great](https://twitter.com/se_hampton/status/719664611235012608), [great](https://twitter.com/ftmaestre/status/718703204813447168).  Whether users are calling it [fresh](https://twitter.com/ValeriaScotti6/status/721058920580689920) or [beautifully crafted](https://twitter.com/stekosteko/status/719753945653100544), or [sleek](https://twitter.com/matthwmaclennan/status/718514893721194496) or [smooth](https://twitter.com/LeonDerczynski/status/718500805159030784) or [snazzy](https://twitter.com/kara_woo/status/719259534250749953), everyone seems to agree that the new version [looks awesome](https://twitter.com/n_simons/status/719336226495418369), it looks [pretty damn awesome](https://twitter.com/Protohedgehog/status/719545053371318272). And we are pretty thrilled to hear that.

They’re enjoying that it’s [got](https://twitter.com/asui085/status/719703892057989121) [some](https://twitter.com/DoctorZen/status/718511708298293248) [fun](https://twitter.com/rdmpage/status/720850590159347712) 🙂 And, we’re not surprised to hear that people [like](https://twitter.com/Roi_Maor/status/719037219458170880) the new price point of [Free](https://twitter.com/CyrilRPernet/status/718674313835102212), making it [easier to recommend](https://twitter.com/sharmanedit/status/718505641644593153) to others.  

## What’s it good for?

Impactstory helps researchers find [impacts of their work beyond just citations](https://twitter.com/skmorgane/status/718519479647404032). People have found mentions they didn’t know about on [Wikipedia](https://twitter.com/sckottie/status/718523025985961984), discussion in [cool blog posts](https://twitter.com/KTInvasion/status/719433206408339456), and [reviews on Faculty of 1000](https://twitter.com/fellgernon/status/719558723010740224). And not just numbers, but [impact across the globe](https://twitter.com/jcanningclode/status/718758947520397312). Not just numbers but *connecting with people:* for instance user [Peter van Heusden tweeted](https://twitter.com/pvanheus/status/719270619871846404), “Using @Impactstory I discovered someone who is consistently promoting work I’m involved in, but who I had no idea existed!”

All this amounts to more than just a [lovely ego boost](https://twitter.com/quicklizard/status/719816923148656640) (although [it’s that too!](https://twitter.com/janetifimust/status/719550980409401344)). People are telling us that it’s motivating them to adopt more Open Science practices like [uploading research slides to a proper repository,](https://twitter.com/biocrusoe/status/719134077186609152) [getting an ORCID](https://twitter.com/allanaaaaaaa/status/718505843868704768),[ adding works to their ORCID](https://twitter.com/juancommander/status/718536988589035520) profile, and [celebrating](https://twitter.com/loladze/status/720986964489416704) [their](https://twitter.com/Protohedgehog/status/719273751892926464) [non-paper](https://twitter.com/MicheleTobias/status/719032147055484928) [publications](https://twitter.com/Science_Open/status/721092841406078976).

## How are you using it?

People are already [sending their Impactstory profiles to their funders](https://twitter.com/michaelhoffman/status/721054214173114368), and [their funders are loving them](https://twitter.com/CrispComm/status/721087712812515328).  Researchers have added their new profile [to](https://twitter.com/fellgernon/status/719558723010740224) [their](https://twitter.com/_NickGolding_/status/719037647390281729) [CV](https://twitter.com/widdowquinn/status/718532555729977344), and are planning on using Impactstory data to [define innovative ‘pathway to impact’ for UK grants](https://twitter.com/winhide/status/718653864413634561) and in [tenure and promotion packets](https://twitter.com/andreaketchum/status/719727979178434561).

Folks are including it [in](https://twitter.com/sharmanedit/status/720265348700766208) [workshops](https://twitter.com/CaltechLibrary/status/720256824709021696).  And even better — [building things](https://twitter.com/getferret/status/720248541923250176) with our [open data](https://twitter.com/AriBFriedman/status/718749868643233792)! Check out the [ferret.io plugin](http://ferret.ai/), it rolled out impactstory support this week and it’s really cool 🙂

## What have we been doing?

We’ve made a bunch of changes this week in response to your feedback:

- imports all your publications, not just DOIs.  Everything on your ORCID profile now displays in your Impactstory profile, and we’re working on getting more openness and altmetrics data
- twitter integration
  - connecting twitter updates your profile pic so you don’t have to fight with gravatar
  - you don’t have to enter email manually–even faster signup
  - we’ll be using your twitter feed for achievements in the future
- there’s a new Open Sesame achievement
- we changed the scores at the top of the profile beside your picture; they are now counts of your achievements
- the achievements and the import process are better documented
- we rolled out dozens of smaller features, usability enhancements, and bugfixes.

And you?  Join in with [everyone showing off their profile](https://twitter.com/search?f=tweets&q=impactstory.org%2Fu&src=typd%20), [spread](https://twitter.com/CisnerosHeredia/status/718731948479418369) [the](https://twitter.com/ftmaestre/status/718703204813447168) [word](https://twitter.com/CyrilRPernet/status/718674313835102212) ([this](https://twitter.com/CisnerosHeredia/status/718731948479418369) [is](https://twitter.com/ftmaestre/status/718703204813447168) [how](https://twitter.com/doug_burke/status/718531949430751232) [we](https://twitter.com/froggleston/status/718527249562083328) [will](https://twitter.com/Protohedgehog/status/718512521263607808) [grow](https://twitter.com/mattcovey/status/718511620746526720)), and if you don’t have a profile, get one, and tell us what you think!
