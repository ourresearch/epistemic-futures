# MEDLINE literature growth chart

## Full text

[![](http://jasonpriem.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/medline-articles-by-year-lg.png "medline-articles-by-year-lg")](http://jasonpriem.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/medline-articles-by-year-lg.png)We all know the volume of scientific literature is growing.  I went looking for an infographic showing this, but wasn’t satisfied with what I found, so I made one, based on the publication dates of articles in [MEDLINE](http://www.nlm.nih.gov/databases/databases_medline.html).

I got [the data](http://jasonpriem.com/share/num-medline-articles-published-by-year.txt) by searching [PubMed](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed) with the query\
`("[year]"[Publication Date])`where \[year\] was each year from 1950-2009. Then I charted the results in [R](http://www.r-project.org/), and resized them in Photoshop.

The data, R code, and images  are all [CC0](http://wiki.creativecommons.org/CC0) (public domain), and can be used wherever and for whatever you fancy.
