# Megasprint!

## Full text

\

As of yesterday, I (Jason) have joined Heather in Vancouver for what we’re calling the Megasprint: two months of 12hr-day, take-no-prisoners, hardcore hacking on total-impact. We’re working toward the mid-September release of our next version, codenamed [Bruce](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Willis) (total-impact sounds like an action movie name…why fight it). 

Bruce will be our first heavily-publicized release (there will be t-shirts!), and will feature collection-level analysis and visualization tools, data from tons of new providers, and support for collections tracking hundreds of thousands of articles, datasets, software projects, and more. And of course the ability to [knock Alan Rickman off a building](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard).

We’re super excited about all we’ll be able to get done in the next, intense two months…stay tuned!
