# New Twitter account

## Full text

\

We created total-impact’s “@totalpimpactdev” Twitter account a while ago, as a way to keep our small group of developers and early users enlooped about changes to the code. Since then, total-impact has matured past the point where only developers care.

So, we’re updating our Twitter handle accordingly: we’re now tweeting from [@totalimpactorg](https://twitter.com/totalimpactorg). If you follow us already, no need to change anything. If you don’t, do! Our codebase and feature list are improving almost daily, and our Twitter feed is a great way to stay up to date.
