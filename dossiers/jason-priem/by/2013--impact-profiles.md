# new release: ImpactStory Profiles

## Full text

\

Your scholarship makes an impact. But if you’re like most of us, that impact isn’t showing up on your publication list. We think that’s broken. Why can’t your online publication list share the *full* story of your impact?

Today we announce the beginning of a solution: **ImpactStory Profiles**.  Researchers can create and share their impact profiles online under a custom URL, creating an [altmetrics-powered CV](http://www.asis.org/Bulletin/Apr-13/AprMay13_Piwowar_Priem.html).  For example, <http://impactstory.org/CarlBoettiger> leads to the impact profile page below:

[![http://impactstory.org/CarlBoettiger](http://impactstory.files.wordpress.com/2013/06/screen-shot-2013-06-17-at-1-25-34-pm.png?w=628&resize=628%2C374)](http://impactstory.org/CarlBoettiger)

 **http://impactstory.org/CarlBoettiger**

We’re still in the early stages of our ImpactStory Profile plans, and we’re excited about what’s coming.  Now’s a great time to claim your URL —  head over and [make an impact profile](http://impactstory.org/).

And as always, we’d love to hear your feedback: tell us what you think (tweet us at [@impactstory](https://twitter.com/impactstory) or write [through the support forum](http://impactstory.uservoice.com/)), and spread the word.

Also in this release:

- improved import through [ORCID](http://orcid.org/)
- improved login system
- lovely new look and feel!

Thanks, and stay tuned… lots of exciting profile features in store in the coming months!
