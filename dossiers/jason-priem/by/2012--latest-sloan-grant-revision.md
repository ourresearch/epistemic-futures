# latest Sloan grant revision

## Full text

\
We’ve submitted a [revision to our Sloan Foundation grant](https://docs.google.com/open?id=0By0SDlWE5_VYV3hvS2FNSzFSSHUtbHlIWWhxUzFIQQ) in response to comments and feedback from them, and to reflect some updated ideas we’ve had.

The biggest change is the budget. I’m close to full-time already because TI is my dissertation. But we’ve boosted Heather’s grant salary to the point where she’d only be 50% supported by her current postdoc, with the [other half by the TI grant](http://wp.me/p2LlD9-F).

(**Update:** we received the grant! [Read all about it here.](http://wp.me/p2LlD9-q))
