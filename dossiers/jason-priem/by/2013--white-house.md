# ImpactStory invited to the White House!

## Full text

\
ImpactStory went to the White House last week!  We were invited to present a poster at the reception following the [Open Science Champions of Change](http://www.whitehouse.gov/champions/open-science) award ceremony.

[![ImpactStory poster at the White House](http://impactstory.files.wordpress.com/2013/06/standing-by-poster.jpg?w=628&resize=402%2C258)](http://impactstory.files.wordpress.com/2013/06/standing-by-poster.jpg)

We were thrilled and honored to represent the future of open science beside Jean-Claude Bradley et al on Open Notebook Science, John Wilbanks et al on Portable Consent, and 9 [other great initiatives](http://www.openscience.org/blog/?p=686).

Tweeting about the invitation wasn’t allowed till the event, otherwise you would have heard all of our excitement — and the excitement of our moms and 7 year olds — much more directly!

Our poster is available [on GitHub](https://github.com/total-impact/white-house-poster) under a CC0 license.

Thank you, OSTP, for recognizing the value of Open Science.  This was a big day for ImpactStory, but a much bigger day for what we’re passionate about: a world of fast, open, web-native scholarship.
