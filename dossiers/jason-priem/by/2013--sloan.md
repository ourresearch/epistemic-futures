# ImpactStory awarded $500k grant from the Sloan Foundation

## Full text

\
![Supported by the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation](https://i0.wp.com/raw.github.com/total-impact/total-impact-webapp/master/totalimpactwebapp/static/img/logos/sloan.png?w=1000&ssl=1)

We’re delighted to announce that ImpactStory has been awarded a \$500k grant from the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation.

Over the next two years, the funds will **“support the scaling and further development to sustainability of ImpactStory, a nonprofit open altmetrics platform that helps scholars evaluate, sort, consume, and reward web-native products.”**

This grant continues the relationship between ImpactStory and [Sloan](http://www.sloan.org/).   ImpactStory was still an evening-and-weekends project running hackathon code when it was [awarded \$125k](http://total-impact.tumblr.com/post/20131290500/total-impact-awarded-125k-sloan-grant) from the Sloan Foundation in 2012.  This initial funding allowed us to incorporate as a stand-alone nonprofit company, develop a scalable open [web application](http://impactstory.org/) (with [context](http://blog.impactstory.org/2012/09/24/introducing-impactstory/), [embeddable widgets](http://blog.impactstory.org/2012/12/17/new-widget-and-api/), and [impact profile pages](http://blog.impactstory.org/2013/06/17/impact-profiles/)), and do outreach for [open altmetrics](http://blog.impactstory.org/2012/06/08/24638498595/).

Thank you, Sloan.  Thanks especially to Program Director [Josh Greenberg](http://www.sloan.org/about-the-foundation/staff-directory/show-staff/show/people/joshua-m-greenberg/) for his advice and encouragement, the grant reviewers for such perceptive feedback, and everyone who wrote us a letter of support.

We are so excited to have this runway!  Hold on to your hats, here we go….
