## Quick bio

Jason Priem is co-founder and CEO of OpenAlex, the nonprofit (formerly named OurResearch) behind the fully-open index of the global research system of the same name; he earlier co-founded Impactstory and Unpaywall and built Depsy and Get The Research. He co-coined the term "altmetrics" (in a 2010 tweet) and lead-authored the 2010 Altmetrics Manifesto during PhD work at UNC-Chapel Hill's School of Information and Library Science, which he left ABD to run Impactstory full-time. Current role verified against the Q2-2026 OpenAlex town hall (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfbXmKieAYg, 2026-04-16).
