# This year is so nuts for having conferences in Portland, OR

## Full text

[](http://www.chesnok.com/daily/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/508038393_7257c1042a_b.png)

**UPDATED! Just added Open Gov West! Rearranged, and just listing these all in order now. And added #140Conf in Vancouver, WA. It’s close enough. 🙂 And just added Digital Journalism Portland.**

For real.

This summer belongs to the nerds, geeks and hackers. I can’t believe that Portland’s tech scene got no love from [Portlandia](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0XM3vWJmpfo) this year. Thank heavens [they got a second season](http://www.oregonlive.com/movies/index.ssf/2011/02/portlandia_renewed_for_second.html)!

I did some research, and found **TWENTY** distinct conferences happening from now through November in the Portland area.

Anyway, there’s a sweet new service that you might not have heard of called [Lanyrd](http://lanyrd.com), and a [quick search over there](http://lanyrd.com/places/portland/) revealed 20 conferences. 

And over the next few days, several people suggested a few more: 

- [Agile Open Northwest 2011](http://www.agileopennorthwest.com/2011/index.php), 8-9 February 2011

- [SearchFest 2011](http://www.sempdx.org/sempdx-events/searchfest-2011/), 23rd February 2011

- [Python Software Foundation Sprint](http://pythonsprints.com/2011/02/15/portland-sprint-urban-airship/), Feb 26th

- [PDX11 Civic Hacking Unconference](http://www.pdx11.org), April 1-2, 2011 (Plans are coming together now.. so pencil it in!)

- [Innotech](http://www.innotechconference.com/pdx/), April 21, 2011

- [TEDxPortland](http://www.tedxportland.com/), April 30, 2011

- [JSConf US 2011](http://jsconf.us), 2nd–3rd May 2011 (Rumors of crazy fun abound for this, also a party open to the public. Epic!)

- [NodeConf 2011](http://nodeconf.com/), 5th May 2011

- [Open Gov West](http://www.opengovwest.org/), 13-14 May 2011

- [Digital Journalism Portland](http://journopdx.com/2011/03/24/announcing-digital-journalism-camp-2011-poll/), 14th May 2011

- [#140Conf Northwest](http://nw2011.140conf.com/call-for-speakers), 19th May 2011

- [WebVisions 2011](http://www.webvisionsevent.com/), 25th–27th May 2011

- [World Domination Summit](http://worlddominationsummit.com/), 4th–5th June 2011

- [HotStorage ’11](http://www.usenix.org/events/hotstorage11/), 14th June 2011 (Third workshop on hot topics in storage!)

- [USENIX ATC ’11](http://www.usenix.org/events/atc11/), 15th–17th June 2011 (USENIX’s annual technical conference)

- [WebApps ’11](http://www.usenix.org/events/webapps11/), 15th–16th June 2011 (Second annual conference from USENIX on webapps!)

- [Open Source Bridge 2011](http://opensourcebridge.org), 21st–24th June 2011 (Third year! CFP still open!)

- [IndieWebCamp](http://indiewebcamp.com/Main_Page), 25th–26th June 2011

- [OSCON 2011](http://www.oscon.com/oscon2011), 25th–29th July 2011 (Back in Portland, Again! And @[gorthx](http://twitter.com/gorthx) is on the committee!)

- [Community Leadership Summit 2011](http://www.communityleadershipsummit.com/), 23rd–24th July 2011

- [Vida Vegan Blog Conference](http://vidavegancon.com/), 26th–28th August 2011 (Blogger conference for vegans! Crazy!)

- [DjangoCon US 2011](http://www.djangoproject.com/weblog/2010/nov/22/djangocon-us-2011/), 6th–8th September 2011 (Organizer recently relocated to Portland!)

- [Pacific NW Drupal Summit](http://pnwdrupalsummit.org/), October 14-16, 2011

- [SPLASH 2011](http://splashcon.org/2011/), 22nd–27th October 2011

- [Onward! 2011](http://onward-conference.org/2011/), 22nd–27th October 2011 (@[al3x](http://twitter.com/al3x/status/35419828923801600) is on the committee for this!)

- [Grace Hopper Celebration of Women in Computing 2011](http://gracehopper.org/2011/), 9th–12th November 2011

Sources: [Lanyrd](http://lanyrd.com), [Plancast](http://plancast.com), [Calagator](http://calagator.org) and the comments.
