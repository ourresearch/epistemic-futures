## Quick bio

Selena Deckelmann is Chief Product and Technology Officer (CPTO) at the Wikimedia Foundation, where she leads the product and technology teams behind Wikipedia and the other Wikimedia projects. She joined the Foundation on 1 August 2022 from Mozilla, where she was Senior Vice President responsible for Firefox (leading ~400 people across desktop, mobile, web platform and browser services), having risen there from crash-data analyst and security engineering manager to Director and then Senior Director of Firefox Runtime. Before Mozilla she was a database consultant and a long-time PostgreSQL contributor and community organizer: founder of PDXPUG, Open Source Bridge, Postgres Open and the PostgreSQL Conference series, a board member of PostgreSQL US, and an advisor/founding board member of the Ada Initiative. Her public writing since 2022 concentrates on Wikimedia annual planning, Wikipedia's role as human-generated knowledge in an internet of machine-generated content, and the load AI crawlers place on Wikimedia infrastructure.

**Role verification (2025–26 sources, high confidence):** she is bylined "Selena Deckelmann, Chief Product & Technology Officer, Wikimedia Foundation" on a Diff post dated [10 December 2025](https://diff.wikimedia.org/2025/12/10/shaping-wikimedia-foundations-2026-2027-annual-goals-key-questions-for-the-wikimedia-movement/), and is introduced as Wikimedia's CTO/CPTO in a [404 Media podcast episode dated 20 January 2026](https://www.404media.co/how-wikipedia-will-survive-in-the-age-of-ai-with-wikipedias-cto-selena-deckelmann/). No departure announcement was found. The roster affiliation ("Wikimedia Foundation") is correct.
