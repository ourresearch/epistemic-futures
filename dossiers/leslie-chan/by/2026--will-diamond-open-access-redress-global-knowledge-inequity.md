Be Compliance, or Be Invisible?
The Digital Object Identifier (DOI) as Governance Infrastructure
• The DOI is not a neutral technology; its inherent power dictates which artifacts gain
recognition (indexing, citation counts, evaluation) and which remain invisible,
making compliance a form of governance.
Participation demands technical
integration, metadata management, staff
time, maintenance, and fees, which are
unevenly distributed and lead to unequal
outcomes.
