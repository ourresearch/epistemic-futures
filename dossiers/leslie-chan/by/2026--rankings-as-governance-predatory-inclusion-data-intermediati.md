Policy directions: refuse auditioning, redesign the rules

• De-authorize rankings in funding & policy decisions
• Require conflict of interest disclosure: ownership, data
suppliers, consulting
• Shift “visibility” to public benefit (not rankability)
• Govern PIDs as contested infrastructure (guardrails)
• Invest in community-governed non-proprietary and
decentralized infrastructure

Don’t optimize for legibility — change the regime.
