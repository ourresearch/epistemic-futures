2.closure and openness are not mutually exclusive or opposite states,
3.there are uneven social consequences and overlooked human costs
to managing processes of opening and closing, and
4.existing systems (technical, political, moral) for regulating acts of
opening and closing serve to bolster privileged commercial,
individual, institutional and social beneficiaries.
