A broad question… Why should we be concerned with the platformitization of infrastructure … And how should we think collectively about building open inclusive infrastructure?

A claim… We have been too focused on the visible problems with Access, and not enough attention to the hidden and invisible problem of Power Imbalance

### Slide 4

Power The ability to set agendas and decisions The ability to mobilize and accumulate resources The ability to set standards and norms The ability to set boundaries of participation The ability to control infrastructure – what gets built
