Thinking beyond the Academies…
Open Science should be seen as a
commitment to opening up the
knowledge production and
legitimation process to include those
who have been traditionally
excluded, under-represented and
marginalized.
• “Openness” is situated and is highly
dependent on historical, political, social,
and economic contexts
• Openness is about the right to participate
in the knowledge production process,
driven by issues that are of local relevance,
rather than agendas imposed from the
outside or from the top down.
Open Science not only calls for
rethinking about the incentive and
reward structure of research funding,
but about who gets to set the
“standards” for the tools and for the
quality of research.
This calls for understanding
new forms of governance,
institutions, infrastructure and
sustainability models. In other
words, new policy thinking
