## Quick bio

Leslie Chan is Associate Professor, Teaching Stream, in the Department of Global Development Studies
at the University of Toronto Scarborough, and director of the [Knowledge Equity Lab](https://knowledgeequitylab.ca/)
([UTSC faculty profile, retrieved 2026-08-13](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/dgds/leslie-chan)). He was one of
the original signatories of the 2002 Budapest Open Access Initiative, and since 2000 has directed
[Bioline International](https://bioline.org.br/), an open-access platform for scientific journals published in the
global South. He was principal investigator of the Open and Collaborative Science in Development Network
(OCSDNet, 2014–2018) and of the Knowledge G.A.P. project on the geopolitics of academic knowledge production.
His scholarly work concerns knowledge equity, cognitive justice, the decolonization of open science, the
governance and enclosure of scholarly infrastructure, and critiques of APC-based open access and of
university rankings and metrics. He sits on the boards of DORA and DOAJ and on the steering committee of
Invest in Open Infrastructure. Earlier in his career he published in primate behaviour and biological
anthropology (1987–1996).
