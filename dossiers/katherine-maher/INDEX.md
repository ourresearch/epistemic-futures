## Quick bio

Katherine Maher (b. 1983) is President and CEO of National Public Radio, a post she has held
since March 2024 and still holds as of mid-2026 ([NPR, 25 June 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/25/g-s1-130271/npr-president-and-ceo-katherine-maher-to-receive-james-c-goodale-first-amendment-award-from-harpers-magazine-foundation)).
She was Executive Director and CEO of the Wikimedia Foundation from 2016 to April 2021, having
joined it as Chief Communications Officer in 2014, and briefly served as CEO of Web Summit
(Oct 2023 – Mar 2024, then non-executive chair). Earlier she worked on digital rights, ICT4D and
democratization at Access Now, the World Bank, the National Democratic Institute and UNICEF.
Her board and fellowship affiliations include chair of the Signal Foundation, the Digital Public
Library of America, the Center for Democracy and Technology, Consumer Reports, Adventure
Scientists, the American University of Beirut, a nonresident senior fellowship at the Atlantic
Council, and 2022–24 service on the U.S. Secretary of State's Foreign Affairs Policy Board
([Knight First Amendment Institute bio, 2024](https://knightcolumbia.org/bios/view/Katherine-Maher)).
Her recurring public subject matter is the knowledge commons: free/open knowledge, verifiability
versus "truth", trust in institutions, internet governance and censorship, and — since 2024 —
the funding and independence of U.S. public media.

### Technique notes for whoever picks this up

**Added 2026-08-14 (cleanup pass):**

- **NYT is fully recoverable from Wayback if you use the right form.** Fetch
  `web.archive.org/web/<14-digit-ts>id_/<url>`, and note the response body is **gzip bytes served
  with no `Content-Encoding` header** — you must sniff `\x1f\x8b` and gunzip manually or the
  extractor sees binary. Then: many captures truncate at the subscription wall in the rendered
  HTML, but the **complete article body is in the page's `window.__preloadedData` JSON**
  (walk it for `ParagraphBlock` / `Heading*Block` nodes and dedupe). That is how the 2025 profile
  and the 2018 Wikipedia piece were recovered after both of their captures looked paywalled.
- **Try more than one capture before calling a URL gone**: the 2024-04-15 tweets story truncates
  in the same-day capture and is complete in the 2024-06-07 one.
- **WSJ is the opposite case** — the archived `__NEXT_DATA__` carries only a two-paragraph snippet
  plus an `encryptedDataHash`, so no capture holds the body.
- `r.jina.ai/<url>` is the workhorse fallback: it defeated PubPub (MIT Press), Politico and Medium,
  all of which 403 scripted clients. It is **blocked outright for `web.archive.org` and
  `www.nytimes.com`** (returns an `AbuseAlleviationError` naming an unrelated prior abuse), so it
  is not a Wayback substitute.
- TED's transcript lives in the page's `__NEXT_DATA__` JSON at

### Ambiguity rulings applied this pass
