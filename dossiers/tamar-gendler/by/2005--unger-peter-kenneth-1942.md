# Unger, Peter Kenneth (1942– )

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

Unger, Peter Kenneth Peter Unger was born on 25 April 1942 in New York City. He graduated from Swarthmore College with a BA in 1962, and went on to the University of Oxford, where he earned his D.Phil. in philosophy in 1966. From 1965 until 1972 Unger taught philosophy at the University of Wisconsin
