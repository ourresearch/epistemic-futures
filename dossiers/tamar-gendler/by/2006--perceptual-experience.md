# Perceptual Experience

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

In the last few years there has been huge philosophical interest in perception. This text showcases the work of fifteen leading philosophers, covering topics to do with sensation and representation, conciousness and awareness and the connections between perception and knowledge and perception and action.
