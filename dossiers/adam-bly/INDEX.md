## Quick bio

Adam Bly is founder and CEO of **System**, a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation whose stated
purpose is "to relate everything, to help the world see and solve anything, as a system" — it
builds the **System Graph**, an AI-extracted knowledge graph whose atomic unit is a
"scientifically-established finding," now focused on healthcare (verified against
[system.com/company/purpose](https://www.system.com/company/purpose) and the
[2026 blog](https://www.system.com/blog), both retrieved 2026-08-13). Before System he was VP of
data at **Spotify** (2015–2017), which arrived through its acquisition of his data-analytics
company **Seed Scientific**; earlier he founded and edited **Seed**, the science-and-culture
magazine (2001–2012), and founded **ScienceBlogs** (2006) and **Visualizing.org** (2010). He was a
Visiting Senior Fellow in Science, Technology & Society at Harvard Kennedy School (2013–2015), a
WEF Young Global Leader, and Vice Chair of the WEF Global Agenda Council on Complex Systems
([Harvard STS](https://sts.hks.harvard.edu/people/fellows/adam-bly/)). He edited *Science Is
Culture* (Harper Perennial, 2010). Born in Montreal, studied at McGill, lives in New York City and
the Hudson Valley.

**Summit-adjacent threads visible in the corpus:** open science and taking scholarly publishing
"out of the hands of publishers" (2012 NASIG keynote); a public-benefit charter committing to a
free, open knowledge base and open, interoperable metadata; AI's effect on how truth is
established, co-written with fellow attendee **Amy Brand** (2023 Boston Globe); rebuilding the
Office of Technology Assessment; institutional trust decay (2024); open data and complexity
governance (2012–2016 blog, WEF work).
