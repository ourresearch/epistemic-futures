# Multilateralism Returns

## Full text

Against the backdrop of epic, global failure in Syria, 2015 was a bright, banner year for multilateralism. The world convened in New York in September to approve the Sustainable Development Goals and set out a comprehensive plan to end poverty and hunger and reduce global inequalities over the next 15 years. In December in Paris, 195 nations signed a landmark climate agreement that will keep the increase in global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels. Earlier this fall, the P5+1 saved the world from a potential nuclear risk in Iran.

In times of rising individualism and complexity, it bodes well for the planet that we still have the courage and capability to come together and solve issues systemically and as one.
