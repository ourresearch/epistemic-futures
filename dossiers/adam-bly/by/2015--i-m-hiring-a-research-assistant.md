# I’m hiring a Research Assistant

## Full text

I am looking to hire a part-time Research Assistant to work with me on a special book project.

You have studied or are well-read in the history and philosophy of science

You are passionate about the relationship between science and society

You have excellent written communication skills

You are a diligent researcher

You are ideally based in New York City

To apply, please send your resume and a cover letter addressing the points above to [ab@adambly.com](mailto:ab@adambly.com).
