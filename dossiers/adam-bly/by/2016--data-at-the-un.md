# Data at the UN

## Full text

Over two years at Seed Scientific, we designed and implemented a strategy — called MY World — to incorporate real-time data into the Sustainable Development Goals process. This was unprecedented in numerous ways and required technological and behavioral change. Now, with the Global Goals successfully in place, the UN has started to evaluate what role data played in achieving this outcome. Says Ban Ki-moon:

“MY World has shown how international organizations, together with civil society groups, can use data to feed people’s perceptions and priorities into the heart of the political process.” — Ban Ki-moon, UN Secretary-General, 2015

I think we are witnessing the birth of a new mechanism for modern global governance: data-mediated, individual-level participation in global deliberations. The implications of this innovation are, I believe, potentially quite profound.
