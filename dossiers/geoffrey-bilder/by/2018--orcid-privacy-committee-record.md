## Full text

Privacy Committee
ORCID Committee                                                                         2012

Privacy Committee:

The remit of the privacy committee was to identify and review privacy issues in light of emergent
business model, system development, and ORCID Principles, and recommend Privacy Policies
to Board for adoption subject to legal vetting.
