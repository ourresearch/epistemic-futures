## Crossref Labs

The other day [Noel O’Boyle](http://www.redbrick.dcu.ie/~noel/) wrote to tell me that he had updated the Ubiquity plug-in that we had developed in order to to make it work with the latest version of Firefox. The problem was, I had \*also\* updated the Ubiquity plug-in, but I hadn’t really indicated to anybody how they could find updates to the plug-in. /me=embarrassed. So it seemed time to provide a home for some of the prototypes and experiments that we’ve been developing at Crossref. To that end, we have created a [Crossref Labs](https://www.crossref.org/labs/) site. Here you can find links to various tools and services that either make it easier to use Crossref services (e.g. [Blog](https://www.crossref.org/labs/wordpress-moveable-type-plugins/)/[Ubiquity](https://www.crossref.org/labs/ubiquity-plugin/) plugins and [OpenSearch Description files](https://www.crossref.org/labs/)) or that serve to illustrate a concept that has been of interest to our members ([InChI lookup](https://www.crossref.org/labs/inchi-lookup/), [TOI-DOIs](https://www.crossref.org/labs/toi-doi-i-e-short-dois/)). Oh, yeah- and when we update these experiments, you should be able to find the updates on their respective pages. Sorry about that Noel… Finally, I will quote from the [Crossref Labs home page](https://www.crossref.org/labs/):

> “Most of the experiments linked to here are running on R&D equipment in a non-production environment. They may disappear without warning and/or perform erratically. If one of them isn’t working for some reason, come back later and try again.” Have fun.
