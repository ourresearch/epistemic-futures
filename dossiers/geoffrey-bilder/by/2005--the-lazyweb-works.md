# The lazyweb works

## Full text

After my trash-talk about “uber-geeks”, [Leigh Dodds](http://www.ldodds.com/blog/archives/000217.html) picked up the “[Subscribe To My Brain](http://www.gbilder.com/blog/?p=40)” challenge and produced [this](http://www.ldodds.com/foaf/brain-subscribe) within hours. He even produced a button, which -as everybody knows- automatically turns beta software into a production service…

It turns out that [Phil Wilson](http://philwilson.org/blog/2005/06/im-trying-to-subscribe-to-your-brain.html), was working on a similar concept and [Danny Ayers](http://dannyayers.com/archives/2005/06/19/group-brain-subscription/) was thinking along the same lines.

And I am happy to say that the phrase “subscribe to my brain” seems to have turned into a mini-meme (Play Austin Powers theme tune here, start maniacally laughing).
