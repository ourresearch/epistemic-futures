## QR Codes and DOIs

Inspired by [Google’s recent promotion of QR Codes](http://www.techcrunch.com/2009/12/06/google-local-maps-qr-code/), I thought it might be fun to experiment with encoding a Crossref DOI and a bit of metadata into one of the critters. I’ve put a [short write-up of the experiment](https://www.crossref.org/labs/qr-code-generator/) on the [Crossref Labs](https://www.crossref.org/labs/) site, which includes a demonstration of how you can generate a [QR Code](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_Code) for any given Crossref DOI. Put them on postcards and send them to your friends for the holidays. Tattoo them on your pets. The possibilities are endless.
