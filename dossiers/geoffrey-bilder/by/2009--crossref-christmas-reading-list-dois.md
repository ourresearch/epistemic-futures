## A Christmas Reading List… with DOIs

Was outraged (outraged, I tell you) that one of my favorite online comics, [PhD](http://www.phdcomics.com/comics.php), didn’t include DOIs in [their recent bibliography of Christmas-related citations.](http://www.phdcomics.com/comics/archive.php?comicid=1262). So I’ve compiled them below.

We care about these things so that you don’t have to. Bet you will sleep better at night knowing this.

Or perhaps not…
