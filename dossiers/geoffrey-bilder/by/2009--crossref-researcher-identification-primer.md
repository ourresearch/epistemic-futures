## Researcher Identification Primer

[Discussions around “contributor Ids”](https://cameronneylon.net/blog/a-specialist-openid-service-to-provide-unique-researcher-ids/) (aka “Author ID, Researcher ID, etc.) seem to be becoming quite popular. In [the interview](https://web.archive.org/web/20091225201433/http://network.nature.com/people/mfenner/blog/2009/02/17/interview-with-geoffrey-bilder) that I pointed to in my [last post](/blog/an-interview-about-author-ids/), I mentioned that Crossref has been talking with a group of researchers who were very interested in creating some sort of authenticated contributor ID as a mechanism for controlling who gets trusted access to sensitive genome-wide aggregate genotype data.

Well, I’m delighted to say that said group of researchers(at the [GEN2PHEN](http://www.gen2phen.org/) project) have created a “[Researcher Identification Primer](https://web.archive.org/web/20090418151033/http://www.gen2phen.org/researcher-identification/researcher-identification-primer)” website in which they outline the many use-cases and issues around creating a mechanism for unambiguously identifying and/or authenticating researchers. This looks like a great resource and I expect it will serve as a useful focus for further discussion around the issue.
