## DOIs in an iPhone application

Very cool to see Alexander Griekspoor releasing an iPhone version of his award-winning Papers application. A while ago Alex intigrated DOI metadata lookup into the Mac version of papers and now I can get a silly thrill from seeing Crossref DOIs integrated in an iPhone app. Alex has just posted [a preview video of the iPhone application](https://web.archive.org/web/20100317112846/http://mekentosj.com/papers/iphone/) and it includes a cameo appearance by a DOI. Yay.
