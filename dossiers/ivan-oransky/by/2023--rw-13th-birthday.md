# Happy 13th birthday, Retraction Watch: Another eventful year

## Full text

In our old diner haunt, more than a decade ago

If Retraction Watch were the child of Jewish parents – which happens to be the case – it would be celebrating its bar mitzvah this year. Yes, that’s right, mazel tovs are in order: Retraction Watch turns 13 today. 

And as the blog becomes some version of an adult, we have plenty to celebrate. This week, we published an [op-ed in *Scientific American*](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/science-corrects-itself-right-a-scandal-at-stanford-says-it-doesnt/). And here are highlights of the past 12 months:

- A new [\$250,000 grant from the WoodNext Foundation](https://retractionwatch.com/2022/12/22/retraction-watch-grows-again-thanks-to-a-250000-grant-from-the-woodnext-foundation/) that allowed us to hire another editor, Fred Joelving, who is already [breaking stories right and left](https://retractionwatch.com/author/frederik-joelving/).
- The additional bandwidth got us back to partnering with other outlets. Since January, we’ve published investigations and other stories with [Undark](https://undark.org/2023/05/31/in-the-death-of-an-iranian-scientist-hints-of-unchecked-strife/), [Science](https://www.science.org/content/article/did-nasty-publishing-scheme-help-indian-dental-school-win-high-rankings), [STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2023/06/09/retracted-suicide-risk-study-how-published/), and the Flatwater Free Press (with [pickup by Nebraska Public Media](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/a-cornhusker-concussion-study-appeared-to-accurately-id-head-injuries-then-it-hit-a-brick-wall/)).
- A [study published in Research Policy](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.respol.2023.104815) “found that reporting retractions on RW significantly reduced post-retraction citations of non-swiftly retracted articles in biomedical sciences.” In other words, when Retraction Watch covers a retraction, the retracted paper is less likely to be cited.
- The [Retraction Watch Database](http://retractiondatabase.org/) surpassed 40,000 retractions, which is three times as many as PubMed and multiples of other sources, and powers [scholarship](https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.2119086119) as well as retraction alerts in [Edifix](https://newsroom.wiley.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2023/Wileys-Edifix-Integrates-with-the-Retraction-Watch-Database-to-Improve-Research-Integrity/default.aspx), [EndNote](https://twitter.com/RetractionWatch/status/1458421915761221643), [LibKey](https://mailchi.mp/thirdiron/jan2021_newsletter-1414009), [Papers](https://www.papersapp.com/blog-papers-announces-expanded-retraction-support/), and [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/blog/retracted-item-notifications/). Clarivate has begun using it to [vet its Highly Cited Researchers list](https://retractionwatch.com/2022/11/15/why-misconduct-could-keep-scientists-from-earning-highly-cited-researcher-designations-and-how-our-database-plays-a-part/). The Economist used data from the Retraction Watch Database to [illustrate the amount of fraud in the biomedical literature](https://www.economist.com/science-and-technology/2023/02/22/there-is-a-worrying-amount-of-fraud-in-medical-research).  
- We published an invited editorial in *Anesthesiology*, “[How to Stop the Unknowing Citation of Retracted Papers](https://pubs.asahq.org/anesthesiology/article/137/3/280/136809/How-to-Stop-the-Unknowing-Citation-of-Retracted).”

Our work was cited by or featured in [The Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2023/07/28/metro/studies-retracted-after-harvard-professor-who-researches-honesty-faces-allegations-fraud/), [The Chronicle of Higher Education](https://www.chronicle.com/article/this-questionable-study-caught-fire-in-anti-vaccine-circles-how-did-it-get-through-peer-review), [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2022/oct/11/prestigious-medical-journal-retracts-nine-more-articles-authored-by-concussion-expert-paul-mccrory), [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-02299-w), [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/04/23/world/europe/chinese-censorship-covid.html), [The Philadelphia Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/health/penn-scientist-william-armstead-investigation-retracted-piglets-20220906.html), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/factcheck-ivermectin-covidmortality-idUSL1N30K205), [Science Friday](https://www.sciencefriday.com/segments/scientific-journals-false-claims/), [Times Higher Education](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/japanese-university-president-forced-out-over-self-plagiarism), [Vox](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/2023/7/14/23794186/animal-testing-experiments-vivisection-academic-fraud-dishonesty-research), [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/articles/stanford-presidents-research-draws-concern-from-scientific-journals-11671142757), [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/education/2023/07/31/stanford-president-science-journal-retractions/), [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/scientific-fraud-is-slippery-to-catch-but-easier-to-combat/), and elsewhere. And it’s not just those U.S. and UK-based outlets, but also publications in [Argentina](https://www.clarin.com/sociedad/pidieron-chatgpt-escribiera-paper-cientifico-hizo-papelon_0_LcQEJBcOK8.html), [Bangladesh](https://www.dhakatribune.com/feature/276059/when-a-tweet-exposed-plagiarism-by-bangladeshi), [Denmark](https://www.dagensmedicin.se/specialistomraden/rorelseorganen/visionen-de-vill-skapa-ben-och-brosk-i-laboratoriet/), [France](https://www.lemonde.fr/sciences/article/2023/07/08/les-inconduites-scientifiques-sont-bien-plus-communes-qu-on-veut-bien-l-admettre_6181131_1650684.html), [Germany](https://faktencheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.33DJ3FR), [Hong Kong](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3220810/stem-cell-study-nature-didnt-have-ethical-approval-chinese-academy-says), [India](https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/science/iit-madras-uses-seawater-to-produce-hydrogen-how-space-travel-impact-brains/article66957611.ece), [Israel](https://www.haaretz.co.il/science/2023-04-27/ty-article/.premium/00000187-bea4-d1ac-a987-ffac3c6a0000), [Norway](https://khrono.no/skandaleartiklene-siteres-arevis-etter-at-de-ble-trukket/718129), [The Netherlands](https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2023/01/25/te-koop-een-plekje-op-de-auteurslijst-van-een-wetenschappelijk-artikel-a4155246),  [Vietnam](https://giaoducthudo.giaoducthoidai.vn/tinh-tao-khi-lua-chon-dang-bai-tren-tap-chi-khoa-hoc-quoc-te-16838.html), and elsewhere.
