# Meet the scientific sleuths: More than two dozen who’ve had an impact on the scientific literature

## Full text

[](https://pixabay.com/en/sherlock-holmes-detective-147255/)Over the years, we have written about a number of the sleuths who, on their own time and often at great risks to their careers or finances, have looked for issues in the scientific literature. Here’s a sampling:

- **Anna** **Abalkina**, the creator of the [Retraction Watch Hijacked Journal Checker](https://retractionwatch.com/the-retraction-watch-hijacked-journal-checker/), has revealed [how paper mills work](https://retractionwatch.com/author/anna-abalkina/).

- **David Allison** and **Andrew Brown** tried getting journals to correct or retract two dozen papers with obvious errors. [The results weren’t pretty](https://retractionwatch.com/2016/02/03/six-barriers-to-correcting-the-scientific-record-qa-with-david-allison-and-andrew-brown/).

- 

*Elies Bik*

**Elisabeth “Eagle Eyes” Bik** [showed that one in 25 papers she examined had evidence of inappropriate image manipulation](https://retractionwatch.com/2016/04/19/one-in-25-papers-contains-inappropriately-duplicated-images-screen-finds/). Our 2019 [profile for *The Scientist*](https://www.the-scientist.com/news-opinion/eye-for-manipulation--a-profile-of-elisabeth-bik-65839).

- [**Dorothy Bishop**](https://www.thetransmitter.org/publishing/retraction-she-wrote-dorothy-bishops-life-after-research/)‘s “eye for detail has given her a second career and a new following.”

- **Mark J. Bolland, Alison Avenell, Greg D. Gamble, **and **Andrew Grey** demonstrated that the work of Yoshihiro Sato — who now holds a prominent place on our [leaderboard](https://retractionwatch.com/the-retraction-watch-leaderboard/) — [was deeply flawed](https://retractionwatch.com/2016/11/09/analysis-casts-doubt-on-bone-researchers-body-of-work/). Grey even published a letter about [how bad one of the retraction notices was](https://retractionwatch.com/2018/08/16/dear-editor-your-retraction-notice-stinks/). More on this case [from Kai Kupferschmidt at Science](http://www.sciencemag.org/news/2018/08/researcher-center-epic-fraud-remains-enigma-those-who-exposed-him).

- **Paul Brookes** created [http://Science-Fraud.org](https://t.co/vTg9L3gz49). He had to shut it down after legal threats. But the scientists featured there [have now retracted dozens of papers](https://retractionwatch.com/2014/04/03/does-publicly-questioning-papers-lead-to-more-corrections-and-retractions/).

- **Jennifer Byrne** [became a literature watchdog](https://retractionwatch.com/2017/01/19/turned-cancer-researcher-literature-watchdog/) after she found a bunch of errors in DNA constructs reported by papers. The number of papers that have resulted from her inquiries [keeps climbing](https://retractionwatch.com/2017/09/15/project-fact-check-genetic-studies-leads-three-retractions-just-getting-started/).

- [**Guillaume Cabanac**](https://retractionwatch.com/?s=cabanac) and **[Cyril Labbé](https://retractionwatch.com/2021/07/19/tortured-phrases-lost-in-translation-sleuths-find-even-more-problems-at-journal-that-just-flagged-400-papers/) **have uncovered hundreds of papers with telltale signs that they were created using programs like Scigen and Mathgen, using their [Problematic Paper Screener](https://www.irit.fr/~Guillaume.Cabanac/problematic-paper-screener). [**Alexander Magazinov**](https://retractionwatch.com/?s=magazinov) has joined them to find papers that contain such “tortured phrases,” and Cabanac and Labbé have also [worked with Byrne](https://retractionwatch.com/?s=cyril+byrne) and others on different projects.

- **John Carlisle** was instrumental in [exposing the statistical anomalies in the work of Yoshitaka Fujii](http://nautil.us/issue/24/error/how-the-biggest-fabricator-in-science-got-caught), who tops our [leaderboard](https://retractionwatch.com/the-retraction-watch-leaderboard/) with 183 retractions. [Another of Carlisle’s projects](https://retractionwatch.com/2017/06/05/two-100-clinical-trials-eight-major-journals-likely-contain-inaccurate-data-study/) looked at more than 5,000 clinical trials, and flagged a study in the New England Journal of Medicine that was [retracted and replaced](https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2018/06/13/619619302/researchers-soften-claim-that-mediterranean-diet-prevents-heart-attacks?live=1).

- “From a small town in Wales, a scientific sleuth has shaken Dana-Farber — and elevated the issue of research integrity.” [Meet **Sholto David**](https://www.statnews.com/2024/01/27/sholto-david-profile-dana-farber-retractions/).

- 

*Michael Dougherty*

[**Michael Dougherty**](https://retractionwatch.com/author/michael-dougherty/) has become the [philosophy plagiarism police](https://retractionwatch.com/2018/06/12/philosophers-meet-the-plagiarism-police-michael-dougherty/).

- 
**Malte Elson** and **Patrick Markey** paid a price for [being right about problems in a study of violent video games](https://retractionwatch.com/2018/07/25/meet-two-data-sleuths-who-paid-a-steep-price-for-raising-concerns-about-a-problematic-paper/).

- **Fabrice Frank**,** Nans Florens**,** Gideon Meyerowitz-Katz**,** Jérôme Barriere**,** Éric Billy**,** Véronique Saada**,** Alexander Samuel**,** Jacques Robert and Lonni Besançon have **[taken on the work of Didier Raoult](https://retractionwatch.com/2023/12/01/elsevier-investigating-articles-linked-to-controversial-french-researcher/). Some of these sleuths, along with others, also took on a paper that made [unsubstantiated claims about the risks of COVID-19 vaccines](https://retractionwatch.com/2022/08/22/what-happened-when-we-tried-to-get-a-paper-claiming-billions-of-lives-are-potentially-at-risk-from-covid-19-vaccines-retracted/).

- **[Hampton Gaddy](https://retractionwatch.com/?s=gaddy)** uncovered more than two dozen odd papers about Star Trek in a journal about early human development. They’ve been retracted.

- **James Heathers** — who calls himself a “data thug” — and **Nick Brown** have been central to the [Brian Wansink saga](https://retractionwatch.com/?s=brian+wansink), and have [created tools that others can use to detect problems](http://www.sciencemag.org/news/2018/02/meet-data-thugs-out-expose-shoddy-and-questionable-research).

- In 2015, **Joshua Kalla **and** David Broockman** began to think something [wasn’t right](https://retractionwatch.com/2015/05/20/author-retracts-study-of-changing-minds-on-same-sex-marriage-after-colleague-admits-data-were-faked/) with a paper in Science about how best to change people’s minds about same-sex marriage. [They were right, and the retraction captured international attention](https://retractionwatch.com/2016/04/14/yes-short-conversations-can-reduce-prejudice-qa-with-authors-who-debunked-the-lacour-paper/).

- [**John Loadsman**](https://retractionwatch.com/2021/06/10/meet-a-sleuth-whose-work-has-led-to-the-identification-of-hundreds-of-fraudulent-papers/) has [identified numerous cases of misconduct](https://retractionwatch.com/2018/10/31/caveat-scriptor-how-a-journal-editor-unraveled-the-mystery-of-the-overlapping-bad-data/) in the [anesthesiology literature](https://associationofanaesthetists-publications.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/anae.14484?af=R), including a case in which a researcher was found to have committed misconduct in [more than 140 papers](https://retractionwatch.com/2021/05/28/anesthesiology-researcher-guilty-of-misconduct-in-more-than-140-papers-investigation/).

- **Ben Mol **has been making a mark, [particularly in the obstetrics and gynecology literature](https://retractionwatch.com/?s=%22ben+mol%22)**.**

- **Leif Nelson**,** Uri Simonsohn**, and** Joe Simmons** — the scientists behind [Data Colada](https://datacolada.org/) — have found fatal flaws in [high-profile studies of behavior and related subjects](https://www.wsj.com/science/data-colada-debunk-stanford-president-research-14664f3?st=en2z1fzrmubmey3&reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink), including in the work of Francesca Gino — who has [sued them](https://www.chronicle.com/article/when-scholars-sue-their-accusers).

- **Michèle B. Nuijten**, along with **Chris Hartgerink**, [created “statcheck,”](https://retractionwatch.com/2015/11/17/making-it-easier-and-more-automated-to-find-errors-a-guest-post-from-the-co-developer-of-statcheck/) which automatically spots statistical mistakes in psychology papers, making it significantly easier to find flaws.

- The grad student who raised concerns about the work of Cornell psychology researcher Robert Sternberg was **Brendan O’Connor**. [Another “data thug” is born](https://retractionwatch.com/2018/05/02/a-new-data-thug-is-born/).

- **Mike Rossner** has made a name for himself as an [image manipulation detective for more than two decades](https://retractionwatch.com/2016/02/24/dont-trust-an-image-a-new-company-can-help/).

- [**David Sanders **](https://retractionwatch.com/?s=david+sanders)was sued for his efforts, and won — but [did not emerge unscathed](https://retractionwatch.com/2021/07/01/a-scientist-critic-was-sued-and-won-but-did-not-emerge-unscathed-this-is-his-story/). 

- **Kyle Sheldrick** has [specialized in COVID-19 and related papers](https://retractionwatch.com/2022/01/19/how-an-ivermectin-study-that-didnt-mention-covid-19-fell-under-scrutiny/), but not exclusively.

- [**Artemisia Stricta**](https://retractionwatch.com/2019/10/15/a-researcher-with-30-retractions-and-counting-the-whistleblower-speaks/) — a pseudonym — has uncovered [hundreds of problematic papers](https://retractionwatch.com/2020/08/14/the-iran-connection-a-ring-of-four-research-groups-has-published-hundreds-of-dodgy-papers-says-whistleblower/) by researchers including one on our leaderboard.

- **Deborah Weber-Wulff** is a key member of VroniPlag Wiki, [a group of German-language scientists who have been scanning for — and publicly tracking — cases of plagiarism](https://retractionwatch.com/2016/02/11/why-plagiarism-is-such-a-problem-for-german-phds-qa-with-debora-weber-wulff/).

- **Nick Wise**‘s work has led to the [retraction of more than 850 papers](https://retractionwatch.com/2022/10/25/meet-a-sleuth-whose-work-has-resulted-in-more-than-850-retractions/). He now [works at Taylor & Francis](https://retractionwatch.com/2025/02/18/nick-wise-sleuth-publisher-taylor-francis/).
