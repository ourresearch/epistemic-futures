## Quick bio

David C. Krakauer (b. 1967) is President and William H. Miller Professor of Complex Systems at the Santa Fe Institute (president since 2015; verified against his SFI profile, https://www.santafe.edu/people/profile/david-krakauer, and his personal site, https://davidckrakauer.com/about-2, 2026). An evolutionary theorist (DPhil Oxford), his research spans the evolution of intelligence and stupidity, collective computation, information theory of individuality, and — recently and intensively — what LLMs/AI mean for human understanding and knowledge ("exbodiment", the debate over understanding in LLMs, the rise and fall of g in AGI). He is a prolific essayist (Nautilus, Aeon, SFI's Parallax newsletter), editor of SFI Press volumes (Worlds Hidden in Plain Sight, Foundational Papers in Complexity Science), and author of The Complex World (2024).
