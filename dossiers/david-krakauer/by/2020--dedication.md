# Dedication

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

To Murray Gell-Mann (1929–2019) Because without a science designed to take more than a “crude look at the whole” of population health challenges, we have little chance to advance the health of people most in need. ~ YA To David Mendez, Rick Riolo, Carl Simon, Mark Newman, and Scott Page (University of Michigan) Because without trailblazers teaching and building research portfolios using innovative methods, it might not have seemed possible (or nearly as fun). ~ KHL To Donella Meadows (1941–2001) Because without the accessible path laid out by her Thinking in Systems—my first steps into the world of complex systems—my scholarly journey would not have led me here. ~ MKL
