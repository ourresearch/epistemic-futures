## Quick bio

**Alex Springer** (legal name **Joseph Alexander Springer**; GitHub, LinkedIn and email handle
`jalexspringer`) is a London-based technologist working on attribution and transparency standards
for AI content usage. He is **founding director of OpenAttribution Limited**, a UK company limited
by guarantee ([company 17002582](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/17002582/officers),
incorporated 2026-01-30, of which he was the sole director until Florian Gramshammer joined on
2026-05-01), which publishes the **AIMS** agent-identity standard and a commerce profile of the
**Content Telemetry** standard. He is **technical lead and maintainer of Content Telemetry for the
SPUR Coalition** (Standards for Publisher Usage Rights — founded by the BBC, Financial Times,
Guardian Media Group, Mediahuis, Sky News and Telegraph Media Group, later joined by the Associated
Press), a role stated on [contenttelemetry.org](https://contenttelemetry.org/) ("Maintained by Alex
Springer on behalf of the SPUR Coalition") and in
[SPUR's 2026-06-12 press release](https://www.spurcoalition.org/updates/spur-telemetry-standard-published-for-public-comment-the-spur-coalition).
He also founded **NarrativAI Ltd** ([16566726](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/16566726),
incorporated 2025-07-08), a consultancy that operates OpenAttribution's telemetry ingestion service
under contract, and is a director of **Common Measure Ltd** (17363855, appointed 2026-07-27). He
co-leads **the APMA's AI Taskforce** with James Bentley ([APMA, 2026-02-03](https://theapma.co.uk/apma-launches-ai-taskforce-to-tackle-the-future-of-affiliate-marketing-3/)).

Before this he spent roughly a decade in affiliate/partnership marketing technology: **Senior
Director of AI Strategy and Partnerships at impact.com** (the title the APMA used for him in
February 2026), and earlier **VP Sales and Solutions Architects EMEA** (London), **Manager,
Solutions Architects** (New York) and **Enterprise Solutions Architect** (Santa Barbara) at
Impact/Impact Radius from 2015, preceded by Citrix Systems and a marketing role at Ancillary
Medical Solutions ([his own résumé](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jalexspringer/resume-profile/master/source/resume.md)).
He holds AWS Solutions Architect (Associate) and Linux Foundation SysAdmin certifications. He is a
paragliding pilot and wrote a personal flying blog, The Deepline Project, from 2018 to 2020.

### Conventions notes for this dossier

- **Organisational bylines from a one-author organisation.** OpenAttribution's research documents,
  the standards specifications, and the NarrativAI "Approach" page carry **no personal byline**.
  They are filed in `by/` anyway, with `authors:` stating the organisational attribution and a
  `notes:` field spelling out the inference chain (sole director / sole repository contributor /
  sole bylined blog author). This departs from the `tui-shaub` ruling, where an unbylined Omidyar
  document was excluded — the difference is that Omidyar is a 49-person organisation in which she
  had no authorial role, whereas OpenAttribution Limited has exactly one public author. Flagged
  for PILOT-LESSONS.
- **Image-only blog posts get no file.** Five Deepline posts contain nothing but image embeds.
  Rather than create files with empty bodies, they are enumerated above. (This is the image
  analogue of the blessed "post whose payload is a video embed" rule.)
- **Robots/licensing signal recorded, not acted on.** `openattribution.org/robots.txt` carries a
  Cloudflare-managed `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference` header and a
  `Disallow: /` for ClaudeBot and other AI crawlers, plus an RSL `License:` pointer. The material
  here was fetched as user-directed retrieval into a private research corpus, not crawled for
  training, and every item records its `source_url`; the signal is noted here for transparency.
- **AI-assisted commit provenance, recorded neutrally.** Some commits in the SPUR and
  OpenAttribution repositories are authored by "NarrativAI Agent" using his email address. This is
  noted in the `notes:` of the specification items as provenance, without inference about how much
  of any document was drafted that way.
