# Alex Springer

AI Commerce, Content Attribution, Transparency Standards

## About

Alex works on transparency and accountability in AI systems with a focus on how they ingest and use content and skills to deliver users outcomes. Alex has spent over a decade at the intersection of content, commerce, and advertising — building and scaling partnership technology across the US, UK, and Europe. He consults with some of the largest media organisations in the world to develop technical solutions ranging from content access control and monetisation to building accountable agentic workflows.

Alex is a leader on the AI Taskforce for the APMA (Affiliate & Partner Marketing Association) and is a regular speaker on AI commerce, content attribution, and the future of performance marketing.

## OpenAttribution

Alex is the director of OpenAttribution.org , a cross-industry coalition establishing transparency and attribution standards for content usage in AI systems.

OpenAttribution develops two core standards:

- AIMS (Agent Identity Manifest Standard) — Agent identity verification using DID-based manifests, enabling content owners to know which AI systems are accessing their content and why

- Telemetry Standard — Session-based content event tracking that creates an auditable trail from content ingestion through AI-generated recommendations

The initiative is Apache 2.0 licensed with multi-stakeholder governance spanning publishers, platforms, and AI providers. SDKs are available for integration.

## SPUR Coalition

Alex is the technical lead for SPUR (Standards for Publisher Usage Rights), a coalition founded by the BBC, the Financial Times, the Guardian, Sky News, and the Telegraph. SPUR builds shared technical standards so AI developers can access journalism legitimately while publishers keep control of their content and receive fair value.

The coalition’s technical work — inference-time telemetry, compliance scanning, and agent identity — builds on OpenAttribution’s open standards.
