## Quick bio

Amy Brand has been Director and Publisher of the MIT Press since July 2015. A cognitive scientist by
training (PhD, MIT, 1989; BA in linguistics, Barnard College), she worked as an acquisitions editor
at Lawrence Erlbaum and then as cognitive science editor for MIT Press's Bradford Books imprint in
the 1990s, ran business and product development at Crossref (2000–2008), was assistant provost for
faculty appointments and information at Harvard, and served as a vice president at Digital Science
before returning to the Press. She is a co-author of the foundational papers behind the **CRediT**
contributor-roles taxonomy, a founding member of the ORCID board, and co-founder (2019) of the MIT
**Knowledge Futures Group** (PubPub); at the Press she launched the open-access monograph model
**Direct to Open (D2O)** and convened the NSF-funded *Access to Science & Scholarship* workshop and
reports. She was executive producer of the Emmy-nominated documentary *Picture a Scientist* (2020)
and of the short *The Uprising* (2021). Current role verified against
[her July 2026 Research Information interview](https://www.researchinformation.info/analysis-opinion/amy-brand-publishing-for-a-new-era/)
and [her Cornell Digital Life Seminar listing, Nov 2025](https://dli.tech.cornell.edu/seminars/digital-life-seminar-amy-brand).
