# NP2020_The Other I-Word: Infrastructure and the Future of Knowledge

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

Slides from opening keynote at NISO Plus 2020<br>Description: The current point in time in the information ecosystem is one in which trust and facts and evidence are an increasingly precious currency. Healthy resilient ecosystems require cross-pollination, and this talk will discuss the importance of diversified and distributed infrastructures and how seemingly divergent stakeholders and models ideally interoperate in our print-optional post-truth world.
