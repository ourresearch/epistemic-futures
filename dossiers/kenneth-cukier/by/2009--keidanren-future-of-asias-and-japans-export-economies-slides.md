Part I: What just happened?!

What does an economic crisis look like?

Japan’s banking system was unscathed
but the drop in demand hit exports
- exports down appx 40% y/y
- corporate losses (eg, Toyota)
- first trade deficit in a quarter-century
- industrial production back to 1980s
- est. 2009 GDP contraction of 3%-6% (worst in OECD)
- deflation! prices falling 0.3% m/m
