# Video & audio — Kenneth Cukier

Noted, not transcribed (per CONVENTIONS.md: a separate later pass handles transcription).
Where an **official text transcript already exists**, the item was saved in `by/` instead and is
marked below with "→ transcript in by/".

Sources for this list: Apple Podcasts search API (`Kenneth Cukier` / `Kenn Cukier`, 2026-08-13),
the media/reviews page of framers-book.com, the Speaking and Media pages of cukier.com, ted.com,
the Keen On Substack archive API and the AI and Faith WordPress search API (both 2026-08-13).
It is a floor, not a ceiling — his conference/keynote footprint is much larger than what is
enumerated here (see INDEX.md coverage notes).

**Transcript sweep, 2026-08-13.** Every show above was resolved to its RSS feed through the iTunes
lookup API and the feeds were checked for Podcasting-2.0 `<podcast:transcript>` enclosures. Only
four feeds publish transcripts at all, and only one of them does so on a Cukier episode:
*Technologies Impacting Society* (2020-12-16), now saved as
`by/2020--technologies-impacting-society-privacy-and-surveillance-transcript.md`. The Keen On,
Innovation Show and Deep Dive feeds carry transcripts on other episodes but not on his. Episode
pages were also checked by hand for Thee Quaker Podcast, the AI and Faith podcast, Info-Tech's
Digital Disruption and iai.tv: none publishes a transcript. TED and NPR remain the only other two
transcript conversions, both made in the previous pass.

## Podcast hosting (his own)

- **Babbage** — The Economist's weekly science-and-technology podcast. Cukier hosted/co-hosted it for
  several years (he is described as "host of its weekly tech podcast" in the 2018 Chatham House
  contributor note and as "cohost of its weekly tech podcast Babbage" in a Jan-2024 Keen On
  episode note); Alok Jha is the host in the 2024+ episodes of the public feed.
  Show feed: https://feeds.acast.com/public/shows/e421d786-ec36-4148-aa99-7a3b2928a779 —
  **the public feed is truncated to 11 promotional/sample episodes**, so his episode-by-episode
  hosting record is NOT enumerable from it. The full back catalogue sits behind
  economist.com's paywall (which also 403s non-browser clients). No published transcripts were
  found. This is the single biggest gap in the corpus; see INDEX.md coverage notes.
- One Babbage episode surfaced in the Apple search and is listed above: "Babbage: Science book club"
  (2023-12-20, Economist Podcasts feed).
