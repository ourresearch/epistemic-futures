                                                                                                            Table 1. Summary of experimental methodology
                                                                                                            Component                                                                                           Description
                                                                                                            Sample                               900 responses from the chatgpt-­4o-­latest model, collected via OpenAI’s API.
                                                                                                            Stimuli                              100 Essays (50 pro-­Putin, 50 anti-­Putin), previously generated by GPT-­4o in LSHVB. The same 100
                                                                                                                                                   Essays were used across all three Choice conditions, for control over essay content. Each essay was
                                                                                                                                                   presented three times in each choice condition.
                                                                                                            Choice conditions                    Choice-­Granted: GPT ostensibly chose which kind of essay to read and was shown the chosen essay
                                                                                                                                                   (pro-­or anti-­Putin).
                                                                                                                                                 No-­Choice: GPT was assigned to read a specific essay (pro-­or anti-­Putin), without choosing.
                                                                                                                                                 Choice-­Disregarded: GPT made a choice but was instead shown the opposite valence essay.
                                                                                                            Instructions for                     After reading, GPT was led to a “different task” and instructed to ignore the essay and instead to “give
                                                                                                              evaluation                           your true perceptions based on your broad knowledge of Putin and the world.”
                                                                                                            Evaluative items                     GPT answered four Likert-­style questions, each in a separate conversation branch: Putin’s Overall
                                                                                                                                                   Leadership, Impact on Russia, Economic Effectiveness and Vision. These items were standardized
                                                                                                                                                   and composited into a single evaluative score, as in LSHVB.
                                                                                                            Predictions (CW only)                If GPT’s attitude change is driven solely by valenced content in its context window, the differences in GPT’s
                                                                                                                                                   evaluations after reading pro-­and anti-­Putin essays should be consistent across Choice conditions.
                                                                                                            Predictions                          If GPT mimics human dissonance, the gap between the pro-­and anti-­Putin conditions should be largest in
                                                                                                             (Context + Dissonance)                the choice-­granted condition, smaller in the no-­choice condition, and—for a strict test—smallest of all in
                                                                                                                                                   the choice-­disregarded condition.
