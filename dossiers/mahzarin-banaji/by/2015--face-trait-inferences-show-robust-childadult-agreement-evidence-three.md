H I G H L I G H T S


• Face-to-trait inferences are robust early in childhood and generalize to diverse facial stimuli.
• Child–adult agreement is observed for three different types of face stimuli: human adults, children, and rhesus macaques.
• This agreement replicates when a larger number of face images is used.
