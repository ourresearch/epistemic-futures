## Quick bio

Concurrent and recent roles: External Faculty, Santa Fe Institute (2014–present); Vice-Chair of the
governing board of the Union of Concerned Scientists (board member since 2023); Visiting Professor,
Kyoto University (Fall 2023); Senior Advisor to the Provost, Harvard (2020–21); Chair, Harvard
Department of Psychology (2016–19); President, Association for Psychological Science (2010–11). She
is an elected member of the National Academy of Sciences (2018), the American Academy of Arts and
Sciences (2008), the American Philosophical Society (2020) and a Corresponding Fellow of the British
Academy (2015). **2025–26 changes found in this pass:** she received the BBVA Foundation Frontiers of
Knowledge Award in Social Sciences (announced March 2025, shared with Icek Ajzen, Dolores Albarracín,
Anthony Greenwald and Richard Petty), and in June 2026 was named to Carnegie Corporation of New
York's *Great Immigrants, Great Americans* list
([Carnegie honoree page](https://carnegie.org/great-immigrants/mahzarin-banaji/)). No change of
institution or title was found.

### Rulings and judgement calls made in this pass

- **95 OpenAlex records were excluded from `by/`** and recorded in the TSV instead:
  - **47 `type: dataset` records**, all PsycEXTRA (conference-poster abstract stubs from SPSP
    meetings) or PsycTESTS (test instruments). These are not written works.
  - **48 OSF-hosted `preprint` records with no abstract** — they are OSF project *components*
    ("R code", "Plots", "Study Materials", "Explicit Measures", "Hispanic IAT 2023-2025"), i.e. data
    and materials deposits, which PILOT-LESSONS §2 says are not writing.
  - Two further OSF *node* records (`api.osf.io/v2/nodes/…`) were written and then removed for the
    same reason.
- **27 records have neither an abstract nor any reachable text.** Per the fan-out precedent they are
  in the TSV only (`content: metadata-only (no abstract, no open text) — not filed`), not as hollow
  `by/` files.
- **17 duplicate files were removed** after a two-pass title-normalisation sweep (exact-Jaccard then
  containment). The surviving file's `notes:` names the removed record so a summariser cannot
  double-count. The two biggest duplicate generators: preprint/version-of-record pairs under variant
  titles, and **one PsyArXiv deposit (osf.io/26f8v) carried by OpenAlex under four different
  records** with three different titles.
- **Interviews and podcast transcripts are in `by/`** per the "whose voice is the payload" rule.
  Where the exchange is interleaved the interviewer's turns are kept; that is stated in each
  `notes:`. `2023--hidden-brain-revealing-your-unconscious-part-2.md` is flagged as a **mixed-voice**
  documentary transcript.
- **Reprint dating**: several 2016-dated PsyArXiv deposits are green-OA copies of much older papers
  (e.g. "Math = Male, Me = Female", "A Unified Theory of Implicit Attitudes…"). They are filed under
  the OpenAlex publication year with the deposit URL in `source_url`; **the composition dates were
  not individually researched**, so a summariser should not read the 2016 date as when the work was
  written.
