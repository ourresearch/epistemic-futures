The key to avoiding loneliness is

a lot of online communication with
little substance can sometimes make us feel more alone.
Facebook can help us keep in touch with our friends, but
we too often use it as a substitute for spending face-to-face
time with them. And since we feel the need to keep up with
them online, we don't find moments of solitude where we can
gather our thoughts and learn to be alone. This is perhaps
the greatest cost of constant connection. •
