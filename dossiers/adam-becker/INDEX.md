## Quick bio

Adam Becker is a freelance science journalist and author with a PhD in computational cosmology
from the University of Michigan (2012), where he worked with Dragan Huterer on primordial
non-Gaussianity. He is the author of *What Is Real? The Unfinished Quest for the Meaning of
Quantum Physics* (Basic Books, 2018) and *More Everything Forever: AI Overlords, Space Empires,
and Silicon Valley's Crusade to Control the Fate of Humanity* (Basic Books, 2025), and since
April 2026 hosts the weekly interview podcast *Dreaming Against the Machine* (Multitude). He has
written for The New York Times, The Atlantic, The Guardian, Fortune, BBC Earth/Future, NPR,
Scientific American, Quanta, Undark, New Scientist, and NOVA. He has held positions as a Science
Journalism Fellow at the Santa Fe Institute, Science Communicator in Residence at the Simons
Institute for the Theory of Computing (UC Berkeley), visiting researcher in Logic and Philosophy
of Science at UC Irvine, and visiting scholar at Berkeley's Office for History of Science and
Technology. He lives in Oakland, California.
