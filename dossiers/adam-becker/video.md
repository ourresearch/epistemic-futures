## Podcast he hosts: *Dreaming Against the Machine* (Multitude, 2026– )

Weekly interview podcast hosted by Becker; described on its own feed as "a podcast about
envisioning a realistic and hopeful future … In a world where tech oligarchs and their power
fantasies are driving visions of the future, Dreaming Against the Machine aims to take back the
terms of the public conversation about what our world can and should be." Feeds:
