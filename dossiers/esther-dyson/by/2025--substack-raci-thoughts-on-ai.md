# RACI thoughts on AI

## Full text

One thing I have discovered as I write about humans and AI is the RACI matrix:

**Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed**

I’ll just defer to Google’s AI Overview:

*A RACI matrix is a project management tool that defines and clarifies team roles and responsibilities for each task. It stands for Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed. It helps ensure that everyone knows who is doing the work, who is responsible for the outcome, who needs to be consulted, and who needs to be kept informed.*

*Here's a more detailed explanation of each role:*

*• Responsible: The individual or team that performs the work or task.*

*• Accountable: The individual or team ultimately responsible for the success or failure of the task. There should be only one accountable person or team for each task.*

*• Consulted: Individuals or teams whose input and expertise are needed to inform decisions or perform the work.*

*• Informed: Individuals or teams who need to be kept up-to-date on the progress and status of the task. (Do I owe Google a license fee?)*

Interestingly, all these roles except one could also be filled by an AI. That role is accountability – legally, the person who gets fired or sued when something goes wrong. There’s an implicit notion of moral culpability. And it’s a good way to think about AI. It is truly just following orders, however obscure those orders might be. It is humans’ moral duty to know – and be accountable for – what they are asking for.

Oh, and a punchline: There’s also CAIRO: the same letters in a different order, with “Omitted” added. That could either be the person who falls off the cc list once the team goes rogue, or it could be the CEO’s nephew who doesn’t really do much at all.
