**Esther Dyson** [00:00]: Hi, I'm Esther Dyson and I spoke at the IAB Congress today. My basic message to advertisers, agencies, people delivering messages is that what they should actually be doing is listening harder. The consumer wants attention. He does not want to give attention. So the first thing we did in my session was we turned up the lights so that we, Lisbeth Kirk and I, could actually see the audience ourselves because you want the communication to be two way, not one way. The real trick is understanding that nine times out of ten consumers don't really want to hear from you. They want to hear from other people. So to a large extent, the business of advertising is changing in something more like public relations where you try and find the influencers. In the old days they were journalists, but now they could be anybody. They could be the best bicyclist, they could be someone who's known for their taste in movies, they could be the most tasteful people in terms of clothing. And you want to influence them and then help them spread your message to that broader audience you're actually trying to reach.
