# Hybrid All the Way Down

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

In this chapter, expanding on Davis Baird’s views, I argue that instruments have always been a crucial, albeit often-ignored, element of inquiry. Hence, as far as a third branch of scientific inquiry goes, instruments have always been there, ready to take on their indispensable epistemic role. If we acknowledge that computer simulations are not easily boxed into a single category and do in fact elicit some kind of ‘in-betweenness’ then they can be said to be hybrid instruments. I also provide a detailed overview of what this means in the context of distinct taxonomical considerations of instrumentation from the philosophy of technology and in particular the philosophy of technical artifacts.
