# Computer Simulations in Science

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

This chapter presents a brief and simple overview of the different technical understandings and types of computer simulations. While it offers brief descriptions of widespread types of computer simulations, such as agent-based and equation-based simulations, it also includes an overview of more complex kinds of simulations such as those constructed within supercomputing clusters and those assisted by modern developments in artificial intelligence technologies such as machine learning.
