# Conclusion

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

The concluding paragraphs of this book emphasize the unificatory and explanatory prowess of the instrument view of computer simulations. In particular, it highlights that this one ontological commitment regarding the nature of computer simulations as instruments can unify the many observations made by previous frameworks as well as solve some of the conundrums that originated in the dichotomous historical debate regarding the nature and properties of computer simulations. It also highlights the fact that unlike other accounts of computer simulations from the perspective of the philosophy of science, this book integrates important insights from the philosophy of technology and the history of science.
