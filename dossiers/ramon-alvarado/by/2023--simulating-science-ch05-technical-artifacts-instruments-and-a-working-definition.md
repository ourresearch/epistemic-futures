# Technical Artifacts, Instruments and a Working Definition for Computer Simulations

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

In this chapter, the concepts of technical artifacts and instruments are explored. A definition and analysis of each is given in order to provide the initial conceptual elements of a working definition of computer simulations as instruments. In particular, details, concepts and distinctions from the history of science and the philosophy of technology are deployed to provide a richer and novel approach to the understanding of instruments as technical artifacts, scientific instruments in particular, and computer simulations as such. Key distinctions are highlighted between pseudo-artifacts, artifacts and technical artifacts as well as between instruments simpliciter and scientific instruments. And finally, the unificatory and explanatory features of the definition of computer simulations provided are explored.
