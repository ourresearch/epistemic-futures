# Introduction: Instruments of Futures Past

## Abstract (only openly available portion)

This introductory chapter presents the main theme and questions of this book as well as its methodology, which—unlike other accounts from the perspective of the philosophy of science—integrates insights from the philosophy of technology, the history of science and interdisciplinary literature from the field of science and technology studies. Some of the introductory questions in this chapter include what are computer simulations, how should we understand their role in scientific inquiry, and whether or not and if so, how they should be trusted. The chapter introduces all these elements via two brief stories: one concerning the historical details of the sanctioning of the telescope as a scientific instrument, and another concerning a fictitious future civilization successfully employing electronic black boxes to predict and harness the future.
