# MIT notices Attention

## Full text

MIT Technology Review just did [a nice article about attention](http://www.technologyreview.com/BizTech-R&D/wtr_16698,295,p1.html), focusing on the presentations at [ETech](http://conferences.oreilly.com/etech), starting with Seth Goldstein's work on [Attention Trust](http://www.attentiontrust.org) and [Root Markets](http://www.root.net/): 

> "I was working with Josh Schachter in 2004 and heard him summarize what a tag was," says Goldstein, refering to the human-applied word label used to sort Internet content on Schachter's site, [. "He said a tag is just crystallized attention, and it stuck with me."](http://del.icio.us>del.icio.us)

"Attention" is one of the important vectors for thinking about "collective intelligence," which, as I've just noted, is really the lynchpin of web 2.0.
