# Conversations with AI

## Full text

I’ve been using substack as a reader for years, and I publish on two other substacks, the O’Reilly Radar, my company space for sharing information about emerging technology, and Asimov’s Addendum, my substack for sharing my thoughts about AI governance. But now as a result of an extraordinary conversation with Claude (which will be my next post), I decided to start publishing here. 

I believe that conversations with AI are something profoundly new. I have long said that “AI is a mirror, not a master,” but when I used to say it, I was thinking about how it shows us our biases. “AI is not biased. We are. It’s learned its biases from our training data.” But some recent conversations with Claude have led to me understand that there’s far more to it than that. 

Conversations with AI can reveal much more about our humanity than just our biases. That’s the space I intend to explore here. I will share conversations with AI (mostly Claude at the moment, but sometimes other models), sometimes in my voice, sometimes in the chatbot’s own. I’ll also share reflections on related research and writings that explore the space between humans and AI and the new culture we are building there.

I want to hear from you, to spark conversations that I can use to spark further conversations. I would like to bring you into my conversations with AI, because as Henry Farrell, Alison Gopnik, Cosma Shalizi, and James Evans have said, “AI is a social and cultural technology.” That’s what we’re talking about here.

I will probably post irregularly, when I have a particularly thought-provoking conversation with AI. But as they say, “the tale grows in the telling,” and so postings may grow more frequent over time.
