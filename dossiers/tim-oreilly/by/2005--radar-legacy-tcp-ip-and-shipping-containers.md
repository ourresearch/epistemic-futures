# TCP/IP and Shipping Containers

## Full text

A year ago, at a private meeting at [Warburg Pincus](http://www.warburgpincus.com), I heard Nick Gall of [the Meta Group](http://www.metagroup.com) give a great talk on why TCP/IP is like container shipping -- both were initiated about thirty years ago, revolutionized their industries, and have proven surprisingly robust while other technologies have had to be updated every few years. I was so impressed that I invited Nick to give the same talk at the [O'Reilly Open Source Convention](http://www.oreillynet.com/oscon2005/). Glad to see that ZDnet [blogged the talk](http://blogs.zdnet.com/BTL/?p=1675). Nick gives us some great insight into just why TCP/IP is so durable.
