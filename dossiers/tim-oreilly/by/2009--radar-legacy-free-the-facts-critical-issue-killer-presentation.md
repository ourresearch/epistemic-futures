# Free The Facts: Critical Issue, Killer Presentation

## Full text

[Dave Gray's Free The Facts presentation](http://flickr.com/photos/davegray/sets/72157612691100488/show/) is a must-read, must-share for anyone who cares about either science or open access. 

It's also a masterpiece of presentation economy, and a fantastic demonstration of how to make a text-heavy presentation into something magical. Reminiscent of [the work of Michael Wesch](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGCJ46vyR9o&feature=channel_page). (It's also a fascinating demonstration of the convergence of YouTube, Flickr, and Slideshare as communication and teaching tools, and a foretaste of the generational change that [the New York Times hinted at a few weeks ago](http://www.nytimes.com/2009/01/18/business/media/18ping.html).
