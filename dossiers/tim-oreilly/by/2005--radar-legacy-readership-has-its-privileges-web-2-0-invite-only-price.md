# Readership Has Its Privileges:   Web 2.0 Invite Only Price

## Full text

Gina Blaber, the head of our conference group, writes: 

> Web 2.0 was a huge hit last year, and we expect it to sell out this year. If you want to get in on the early registration pricing, [request an invitation to register](http://www.web2con.com/cs/web2005/create/inv/) before August 16. Also, [book your hotel room soon](http://www.web2con.com/pub/w/40/hotel.html), as we expect that to sell out as well. 

(I had forgotten that there's a period of invitation-only registration before the public registration expires. We've been letting alumni, friends, and other people we want to make sure are part of the conversation register early, and Gina pointed out to me that readers of this blog ought to be allowed to get an early invitation. Sorry for the last minute notice!) Those of you who've been following the [Not 2.0 debate](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2005/08/not_20.html) might want to come see what all the fuss is about.
