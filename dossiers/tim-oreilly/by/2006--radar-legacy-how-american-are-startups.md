# How American Are Startups?

## Full text

I just discovered [the great discussion at plasticbag](http://www.plasticbag.org/archives/2006/05/how_american_are_startups.shtml) of Paul Graham's talk about startups, [How American Are Startups?](http://strange.corante.com/archives/2006/05/17/xtech_2006_paul_graham_how_american_are_startups.php) at [XTech](http://xtech06.usefulinc.com/) back in May. Tom Coates summarized the talk, and then he and his readers went to town critiquing and responding to it. One of the best examples of a meaty, thoughful, and informative blog discussion that I've ever seen.
