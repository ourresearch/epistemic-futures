# Buying the Cow, Though the Milk is Free

## Full text

Publishing industry news site, [The Book Standard](http://www.thebookstandard.com) has just published an article on piracy of electronic books, entitled [Buying the Cow, Though the Milk is Free: Why Some Publishers are Digitizing Themselves](http://www.thebookstandard.com/bookstandard/news/publisher/article_display.jsp?vnu_content_id=1000968186). The article quotes folks like Cory Doctorow, Charlie Stross, Jim Baen, and me talking about how free downloads can be good marketing, or in the worst cases a minor inconvenience, rather than the horror story that the copyright mafia makes them out to be. For my full thinking on this complex subject, I still stand by my 2002 essay, [Piracy is Progressive Taxation](http://www.openp2p.com/pub/a/p2p/2002/12/11/piracy.html).
