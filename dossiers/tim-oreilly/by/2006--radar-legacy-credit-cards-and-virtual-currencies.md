# Credit Cards and Virtual Currencies

## Full text

Over on [Makezine](http://www.makezine.com), Phil Torrone writes about how credit cards are looking to offer [World of Warcraft gold and Linden dollars](http://www.makezine.com/blog/archive/2006/03/the_future_of_credit_cards_ear.html) as a reward for credit card purchases. This is another sign that these virtual currencies are becoming real economies.
