# Just in time for early xmas shopping

## Full text

[Ross Stapleton-Gray](http://www.stapleton-gray.com) writes in email: "Just in time for early xmas shopping...be the first on your block with [a radio-controlled Japanese woman](http://www.forbes.com/technology/2005/08/04/technology-remote-control-humans_cx_lh_0804remotehuman.html)." Whoa. I remember that the Boston Museum of Science used to demo headsets that distorted the timing of your auditory feedback. It was amazing how it was impossible to override the feedback -- I was the demo for my fifth-grade daughter's science class, and I couldn't even recite the alphabet when they turned up the gain. Sounds like this is analogous, but with direct electrical brain stimulation for motor control. Wierd stuff!
