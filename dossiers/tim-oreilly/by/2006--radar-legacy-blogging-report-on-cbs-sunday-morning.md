# Blogging Report on CBS Sunday Morning

## Full text

[David Pogue](http://www.davidpogue.com) writes in email: "Greetings, friend of Pogue... Just a note that my report on the blogging phenomenon is scheduled to be the lead story on "CBS News Sunday Morning" this Sunday, July 9. (As always, this show's schedule may change, but it's looking good.) Set your TiVo!" I'd say that's definitely a sign of mainstream acceptance, and when David Pogue, NY Times technology columnist, Emmy-award winning TV journalist, and bestselling author (of numerous titles in O'Reilly's [Missing Manual](http://www.missingmanuals.com) series and Wiley's [For Dummies](http://www.fordummies.com)) has something to say on a subject, a lot of people pay attention.
