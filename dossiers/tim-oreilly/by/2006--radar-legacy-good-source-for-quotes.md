# Good source for quotes

## Full text

While confirming the source and exact wording of the two quotes I used in the last entry, I discovered a handy site, [brainyquote.com](http://www.brainyquote.com). It's a good resource when you have something in the stacks of your mental library but don't have it exactly to hand. (For example, I had "firm" for "great" in the Jefferson quote, and was about to attribute the Pasteur quote to Pierre Curie.)
