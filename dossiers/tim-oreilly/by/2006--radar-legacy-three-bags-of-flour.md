# Three Bags of Flour

## Full text

The other day, I came back from a couple of days out of the office, and Sandy, my assistant, said, "Oh, and you have these three bags of flour..." I was as puzzled as she was, till I read the letter from Josh Dorf: 

> Over the holidays, I took a bunch of back issues of some magazines I get but don't always read. In a Wired issue, [you talked about baking scones](http://www.wired.com/wired/archive/13.10/oreilly.html?pg=2&topic=oreilly&topic_set=) and so I thought I'd send you some samples of the best baking flour available to consumers. I got out of the whole .com and tech space and bought a 100-year old wheat flour company. I still have an O'Reilly Vi Editor book around here somewhere...." [Link added.] 

Josh may have gotten out of tech, but he clearly knows something about [the cluetrain](http://www.cluetrain.org) and is applying it to his new business. So he clearly deserves a bit of link-love back to his flour company (and he's right, [Stone-Buhr](http://www.stone-buhr.com) is great flour!) Interesting how folks who started in tech have migrated out into old industries, taking with them what they learned. As with all diasporas, I expect a lot of benefit as the two cultures mix.
