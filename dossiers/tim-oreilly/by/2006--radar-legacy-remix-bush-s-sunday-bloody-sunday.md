# Remix:  Bush's Sunday Bloody Sunday

## Full text

[Andy Oram](http://www.oreillynet.com/pub/au/36) writes: "[A brilliant mash-up, illustrating the power of open content](http://pinkdome.com/archives/2006/06/best_video_ever.html). I can't imagine how many hours went into making this." The person who posted the video on YouTube summarizes it thus: "I love the Internet. Here's why. Some genius took Bush's speeches and made them into the greatest version of U2's Sunday Bloody Sunday I've ever heard." 

It is damn good. Not only did I love the remix, I'm going to go out and buy the original song, as I bet many others will also do, as the creative remix adds to, not detracts from the visibility and value of the original.
