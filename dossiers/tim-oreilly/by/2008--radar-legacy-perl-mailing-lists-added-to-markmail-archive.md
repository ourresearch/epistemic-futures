# Perl mailing lists added to Markmail archive

## Full text

Jason Hunter writes in email: 

> Perl is the duct tape of the internet. [Created by Larry Wall in 1987](http://history.perl.org/PerlTimeline.html) and made famous with his [Programming Perl](http://www.oreilly.com/catalog/pperl3/index.html) "camel book" published by O'Reilly, it's the tool sysadmins use to keep things running. 
> 
> We're proud to announce we've finished loading the Perl.org mailing list history into MarkMail. A total of [530,000 emails across 75 lists](http://perl.markmail.org/search/). The lists don't go back to 1987 (boy that'd be cool if they did). But that's all right; who really needs tech support against Perl 1.000? 
> 
> What we have here is traffic starting with the migration to the Perl.org setup in 1999: 
> 
> Enjoy! And if anyone has earlier archives, let us know. 

This is awesome. Markmail also has mailing list history for [php](http://php.markmail.org), [ruby](http://ruby.markmail.org/), [mysql](http://mysql.markmail.org), [postgresql](http://postgresql.markmail.org), and all of the [Apache](http://apache.markmail.org) projects. (Coverage of Python and Linux is very limited.) And as I wrote previously on Radar, the [markmail search and trend graphing interface is really slick](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2008/01/markmail-provides-amazing-sear.html). Google ought to buy these guys and offer this product as an upgrade to Google Trends! Not to mention applying some of their interface insights into other Google search products.
