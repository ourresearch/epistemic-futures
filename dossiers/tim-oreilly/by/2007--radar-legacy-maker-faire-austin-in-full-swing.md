# Maker Faire Austin in Full Swing

## Full text

[Maker Faire Austin](http://www.makerfaire.com) is in full swing. I'm not there, having just finished up the Web 2.0 Summit in San Francisco, recuperating at home before heading out Monday for a MySQL board meeting in London, but I'm watching the goings-on via Flickr, at home via my [chumby](http://www.chumby.com). Chumby is a streaming media device that replaces the old clock radio. You can configure it with all kinds of flash widgets -- great for ambient information. They also support a "virtual chumby" so I can share with you what I'm watching now. Right now, I've got it set to Scott Beale ([laughingsquid](http://www.laughingsquid.com))'s [flickr photo stream from Maker Faire Austin](http://www.flickr.com/photos/laughingsquid/sets/72157602540554260/): 

Update: As Scott wasn't adding more photos as quickly as I thought, I added a photostream of any [photos tagged makerfaireaustin on Flickr](http://www.flickr.com/photos/tags/makerfaireaustin/). (Of course, you can also see them there.) So far it looks like most of the photos are from the setup days. 

Disclosure: [O'Reilly AlphaTech Ventures](http://www.oatv.com) is an investor in Chumby. And of course, MakerFaire is one of our events!
