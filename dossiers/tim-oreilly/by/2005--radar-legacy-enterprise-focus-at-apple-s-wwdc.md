# Enterprise focus at Apple's WWDC

## Full text

At the [Apple World Wide Developer's Conference](http://developer.apple.com/wwdc/), I heard that there's been significant attendee growth vs. last year, and that the driver for the growth was the beefing up of the enterprise track. So it seems that Apple may be making more headway in corporate America than they are normally given credit for. Either that, or a simpler explanation (as one attendee told us): enterprise content is what makes the boss open his budget for a developer to go to a conference. Still, an interesting data point.
