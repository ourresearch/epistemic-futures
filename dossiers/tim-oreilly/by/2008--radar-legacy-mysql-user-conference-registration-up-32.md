# MySQL User Conference Registration Up 32%

## Full text

I was interested to note that as of this morning, attendee registration numbers for the [MySQL User Conference](http://www.mysqluc.com) (which O'Reilly co-produces with MySQL) are up 32% over the same period last year. This seems to be a good sign that the community is energized by [MySQL's acquisition by Sun](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2008/01/sun-to-acquire-mysql.html). 

The conference takes place April 14-17 at the Santa Clara Convention Center. Among other things, we'll be hearing from Jonathan Schwartz, the CEO of Sun, about [Sun's open source strategy](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/detail/2551) (presumably including their plans for MySQL), as well as a deep technical program with tracks including [Storage Engine Development and Optimization](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/29), [MySQL Cluster and High Availability](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/22), [Replication and Scale-Out](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/26), [Security and Database Administration](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/28), [Performance Tuning and Benchmarks](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/24), [Data Warehousing and Business Intelligence](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/17), [Web 2.0, Ajax, and Emerging Technologies](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/30), [MySQL on .Net and Windows](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/23), and of course, [MySQL and PHP](http://en.oreilly.com/mysql2008/public/schedule/topic/25). 

It's shaping up to be a great conference.
