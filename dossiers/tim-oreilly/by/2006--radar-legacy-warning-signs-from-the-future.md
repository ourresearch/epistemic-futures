# Warning Signs from the Future

## Full text

A fun photoset on flickr, pointed out by [Phil Torrone](http://www.flickr.com/photos/87547772@N00/sets/72157594323393196/), shows imaginary [Warning Signs from the Future](http://www.flickr.com/photos/87547772@N00/sets/72157594323393196/). While not all are equally thought-provoking, and some of the things being warned about seem like benefits rather than hazards to me, enough are spot on to make this set worth a visit.
