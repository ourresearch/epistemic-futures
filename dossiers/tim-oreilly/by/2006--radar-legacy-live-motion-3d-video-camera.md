# Live Motion 3D Video Camera

## Full text

The other day, [Noel Gorelick](http://sese.asu.edu/support#NoelGorelick) of Arizona State University and [Google Mars](http://www.google.com/mars/) fame gave me an amazing demo of images taken with a very cool new 3D live motion video camera that uses [LIDAR](http://www.ghcc.msfc.nasa.gov/sparcle/sparcle_tutorial.html) technology to get a range-finding for every pixel. Advanced Scientific Concepts, the company that built the camera, is so young that they don't have a website up, but here's one of the images (moved to the second page because it's an animated GIF):

[](http://www.mars.asu.edu/~gorelick/foo156.gif)

Imagine how a camera like this could be used to populate Second Life or Google Earth! 

The creators of the technology did [a Google Tech Talk about the 3D camera](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=-3656494784112768834&q=3D+Live+Motion+Camera). I haven't watched it yet, but someone who was there said that the last 30-40 minutes are better than the beginning, so if it doesn't catch your attention, skip ahead.
