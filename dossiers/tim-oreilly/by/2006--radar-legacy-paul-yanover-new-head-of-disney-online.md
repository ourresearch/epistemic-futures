# Paul Yanover new head of Disney Online

## Full text

I reported previously on [the interesting conversation about VMK I had with Paul Yanover](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2006/05/disneys_virtual_magic_kingdom.html) when he was the head of [Disney Parks and Resorts Online](http://destinations.disney.go.com/parksandresorts/index?bhcp=1). Paul's a smart guy, and his group has been doing great work, so I was excited to hear that he's been [promoted to head all of Disney's online activities](http://corporate.disney.go.com/wdig/press_releases/2006/2006_0515disney.html). (This actually happened back in mid-May, but it didn't get on my radar till I had a followup conversation with Ed Kummer, who has now succeeded Paul as head of Parks and Resorts Online.) I look forward to seeing great things from [Disney online](http://disney.go.com/home/today/index.html) under Paul's leadership.
