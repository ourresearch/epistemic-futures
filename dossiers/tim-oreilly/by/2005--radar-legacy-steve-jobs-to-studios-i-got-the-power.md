# Steve Jobs to Studios: I Got the Power!

## Full text

Nivi [makes a strong case](http://www.nivi.com/blog/article/steve-jobs-to-studios/trackback/) that Steve Jobs' recent keynote was positioning Apple as a [monopsony](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopsony). This is the inverse of a monopoly, in which one seller has power over many buyers, and instead is a situation where one buyer dominates many sellers: 

> In principle, Steve Jobs' keynote today introduced the iPod nano and iTunes phone.   
> 
> 
> But the real message was directed at his current and potential suppliers (music, movies, components), partners (Sony, Nokia et al), and competitors (Sony, Microsoft et al). 
> 
> What was his message to these colleagues? 
> 
> I buried the competition. 

A terrific, thought-provoking [read about Apple's market power](http://www.nivi.com/blog/article/steve-jobs-to-studios/trackback/) when it comes to digital music, and perhaps, other forms of digital goods.
