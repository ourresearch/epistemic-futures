# Continuing the Conversation with Tim Bray

## Full text

Tim Bray wrote [an extensive reply](http://www.tbray.org/ongoing/When/200x/2005/08/09/Web-2.0) to my [Not Web 2.0?](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2005/08/not_20.html) piece. It's well worth a read (as is [Sam Ruby's piece](http://www.intertwingly.net/blog/2005/08/08/Agile-Web-2-0-Development) on the same subject). Since Tim Bray doesn't allow comments on his blog, I added a substantial reply (maybe longer than my original piece) to the comments on my own blog. I debated making it a new top level blog entry, but there have been so many thoughtful and useful comments posted there that I thought it was better for people to go back and read the reply in the context of [the original piece and all the subsequent comments](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2005/08/not_20.html). Scroll to the bottom if you just want my reply to Tim Bray.
