# Gapingvoid:  Blog as conversational advertising...

## Full text

O'Reilly developer Tony Stubblebine sent me the following comment on my posting, [Remaking Advertising as Conversation](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2005/05/remaking_advert.html): 

> If we remade advertising as conversation would we even recognize it as advertising? ...Terrie [Miller, another oreillynet staffer] turned me on to [a great blog](http://www.gapingvoid.com/) from the PR man for a certain English tailor. His content is mostly snarky comments/comics but his occasional references to the tailor have left me craving a custom tailored suit (I don't wear suits)." 

Tony made a few other comments as well, but they lead right into a longer posting I'm working on about how we're thinking about conversational advertising at O'Reilly, so I'm going to save them for later. Meanwhile, [gapingvoid,](http://www.gapingvoid.com/) a blog about blog as advertising, is a great find. Don't know how I missed it.
