# Steve Jobs' Commencement Speech at Stanford

## Full text

Steve Jobs' recent commencement speech at Stanford was moving and deeply wise. I saw the text when someone posted it to Dave Farber's IP list, and I immediately forwarded it on to my wife and kids, and a moment later, to all my employees. It's a must-read! The official transcript is [available on the Stanford web site](http://news-service.stanford.edu/news/2005/june15/jobs-061505.html).   

If you think at all about how to bring more meaning and passion into your life, read this speech. If you have kids who are old enough to be thinking how to spend their life, give it to them. Just wonderful.
