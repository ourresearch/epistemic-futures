# Jared Diamond at Fort Mason Friday night

## Full text

Stewart Brand sent out the following email announcement about the latest [Long Now](http://www.longnow.org) seminar at Fort Mason: 

> Like Darwin, Jared Diamond is a field biologist who changes people's minds about fundamental matters.� He did it in 1997 about the course of civilization in GUNS, GERMS AND STEEL, and he did it again this year with COLLAPSE, about the ecological fragility of sophisticated societies.� That will be his subject this Friday... 
> 
> Jared Diamond, "How Societies Fail (& sometimes succeed)," 7pm (doors open), Friday, July 15, Cowell Theater, Fort Mason, San Francisco.� The lecture starts promptly at 7:30pm.� Admission is free ($10 donation very welcome, not required). 
> 
> If you would like to be notified by email of forthcoming talks in the series, please contact Simone Davalos--- simone AT longnow.org, 415-561-6582. 

I loved [Guns, Germs, and Steel](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/0393317552/timsarchiv-20), and am very concerned about the issues raised in [Collapse](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-//0670033375/timsarchiv-20), so I'm very much looking forward to this talk!
