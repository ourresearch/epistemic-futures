# Research Fellow opening at Eyebeam

## Full text

Michael Frumin writes in email: 

> the R&D group at Eyebeam has jut put out its first [open call for research fellows](http://eyebeam.org/production/production.php?page=rdfellows), so I�d appreciate it if you took a look and forwarded it to anyone who you think might be interested. 

The stipend won't make anyone rich, but what a chance to work with an amazing group of people who do some of the coolest projects around. If you're a hacker or a maker who likes hands-on projects that make big statements and advance the cause of open systems, [Eyebeam](http://www.eyebeam.org) is a great place to get some experience.
