# Mondo Mobile Market Disruption Coming

## Full text

Nivi posts a thought-provoking list of signals that [the telephony market is headed for mondo disruption](http://www.nivi.com/blog/article/monstro-mobile-market-disruption/). He cites the Apple ROKR as nothing more than "a beta test for Apple to see if they can actually work with the handset manufacturers and mobile carriers", [Series 60 designer Christian Lindholm leaving Nokia for Yahoo!](http://www.christianlindholm.com/christianlindholm/2005/09/i_have_seen_my_.html), and Google’s [acquisition of mobile player Android](http://www.businessweek.com/technology/content/aug2005/tc20050817_0949_tc024.htm) and their [investment in Current](http://news.com.com/Google+invests+in+power-line+broadband/2100-1036_3-5777917.html), a power-line broadband company. 

We also agree that the telephony market is ripe for some creative destruction, which is why we announced [the O'Reilly Emerging Telephony Conference](http://conferences.oreillynet.com/etel/) today. The conference will be held January 24-26 in San Francisco.
