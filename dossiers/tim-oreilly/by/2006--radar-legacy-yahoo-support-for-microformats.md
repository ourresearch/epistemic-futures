# Yahoo! Support for Microformats

## Full text

[Yahoo! Local Blog](http://ylocalblog.com/blog/2006/06/21/we-now-support-microformats/): "Starting today, we’re happy to announce Yahoo! Local fully supports the hCalendar, hCard, and hReview microformats on almost all business listings, search results, events, and reviews." Ultimately, standards for open data interchange (including but not limited to [microformats](http://www.microformats.org)) will turn out to be more important for the web than open APIs or even open source software...
