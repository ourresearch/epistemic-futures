# I was very sorry to see Skyliner close.

## Full text

I was very sorry to see Skyliner close. You created something special, and advanced the art of cloud computing. Being too close to the center of the bullseye is always a dangerous place to be! Your work will be carried on by Amazon. As they say, “Imitation is the sincerest form of flattery.” I hope you and the team rock the world at mailchimp.
