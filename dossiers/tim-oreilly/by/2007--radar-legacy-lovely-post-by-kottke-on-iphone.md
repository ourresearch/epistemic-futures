# Lovely Post by Kottke on iPhone

## Full text

I love Jason Kottke's [post about the iPhone](http://www.kottke.org/07/01/the-apple-iphone). While everyone else is offering opinion, Jason built himself a model out of cardboard, so he could compare its size to other consumer devices. A lovely example of the maker ethic at work.
