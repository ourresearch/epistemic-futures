# SDForum Open Source Panel with Jonathan Schwartz and Rod Smith

## Full text

Next Thursday, January 19, I'm going to be on a panel about open source with Jonathan Schwartz of Sun and Rod Smith of IBM, moderated by John Markoff of the NY Times. The event, sponsored by SDForum, is a brainstorming event about [the future of open source](http://www.sdforum.org/opensource06), and will be held in Santa Clara. I'm a participant, not the moderator, but I imagine there will be some good opportunity to get some spirited conversation going. I'd love your thoughts on what subjects I should be pressing Jonathan and Rod on. What would you ask them if you were in my place? What do you think of their respective open source strategies?
