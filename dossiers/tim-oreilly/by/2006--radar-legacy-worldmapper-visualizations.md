# Worldmapper Visualizations

## Full text

"[Worldmapper](http://www.sasi.group.shef.ac.uk/worldmapper/index.html) is a collection of world maps, where territories are re-sized on each map according to the subject of interest." [[via](http://www.makezine.com/blog/archive/2006/11/worldmapper_the.html)] See how the map of the world changes when you look not just at [land area](http://www.sasi.group.shef.ac.uk/worldmapper/display.php?selected=1), but at other factors, such as [population](http://www.sasi.group.shef.ac.uk/worldmapper/display.php?selected=2), [GDP](http://www.sasi.group.shef.ac.uk/worldmapper/display.php?selected=170), [import/export flows](http://www.sasi.group.shef.ac.uk/worldmapper/textindex/text_goods.html), or [HIV infections](http://www.sasi.group.shef.ac.uk/worldmapper/display.php?selected=227). There are literally hundreds of map projections. This would make a cool demo for [Where 2.0](http://conferences.oreillynet.com/where2006/</a), I think..

If you like this kind of thing, also look at Hans Rosling's amazing visualizations at [gapminder.org](http://www.gapminder.org).
