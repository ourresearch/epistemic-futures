# Playing Flickr

## Full text

When I'm at [EuroOscon](http://conferences.oreilly.com/euroscon), I've got to go check out this [Playing Flickr](http://www.mediamatic.net/artefact-200.9770.html) art installation on the 11th floor of the PostCS building in Amsterdam:   

> The diners in bar/restaurant/club 11 will be subjected to the wrath of fellow visitors SMSing whatever keyword they want to the installation that pulls photos from the online community flickr and projects them onto Restaurant 11's huge panoramic screens. 

Sounds like some of the stuff that [EyeBeam](http://www.eyebeam.org) has done as well. I love the way that technology is starting to power public art.
