# Cory's novel as an RSS feed

## Full text

Cory Doctorow writes in email: 

> This is such [a cool remix](http://www.surfarama.com/index.php?p=242) of my novel [Someone Comes to Town, Someone Leaves Town](http://craphound.com/someone/) \-- an RSS feed that gives you a couple pages every day. No matter when you subscribe to it, it sends you the book starting from the beginning. Subscribe via Winksite and it'll come to your phone in daily bite-sized pieces. 

Love the idea of an RSS feed that always starts at the beginning. This opens up whole new possibilities for publishing.
