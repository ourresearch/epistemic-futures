# Cisco's iPhone Trademark Shaky?

## Full text

Since iPhone postings seem to generate such a comment storm, it's obviously a topic of some interest to our readers. As a result, I thought I should point to [Ed Burnette's argument that Cisco's iPhone trademark may be on shaky ground](http://blogs.zdnet.com/Burnette/?p=236). Apparently, Cisco may have let the trademark lapse, and faked a photo to show it was in use before they'd revived it -- precisely because they knew Apple wanted it. Speculation, and not from a lawyer, but fun for anyone who is interested in such things. [[via](http://paul.kedrosky.com/archives/2007/01/13/issues_with_cis.html)]
