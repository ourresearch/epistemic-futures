# Thought Thieves Film Competition???

## Full text

Microsoft UK has launched a ["Thought Thieves" film competition](http://bink.nu/Article3950.bink) to solicit user-contributed short films "on the theme of 'How intellectual property theft affects both individuals and society'." Seems to me a good opportunity to send them in some short films on the importance of the [creative commons](http://www.creativecommons.org), and how **too much** intellectual property protection harms innovation and otherwise negatively affects both individuals and society.
