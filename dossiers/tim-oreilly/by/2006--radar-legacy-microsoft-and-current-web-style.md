# Microsoft and "current web style"

## Full text

Marc Hedlund noted in email: "It's interesting how closely the [Mix06](http://mix06.com) web site hews to [these observations on current web style](http://www.webdesignfromscratch.com/current-style.cfm): It was *not* like that when it launched." Microsoft has always been a great learning organization when challenged, and the quick evolution of the site about their conference on their "Web 2.0" catchup strategy may be another good sign that they are in fast learning mode.
