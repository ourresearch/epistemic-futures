# bullshitr

## Full text

Don't know why this didn't get noticed before: the excellent [Web 2.0 bullshit generator](http://emptybottle.org/bullshit/). Not as funny as [Web 2.0 or Star Wars character?](http://www.cerado.com/web20quiz.htm), but still worth a spin. In the heyday of the last bubble, I remember going into a conference room in a shared-office complex (so I don't know whose bs it was), seeing the business plan brainstorm on the whiteboard, and thinking that someone ought to come out with a [magnetic poetry kit](http://www.magneticpoetry.com/play.html) (in that case, a magnetic bs kit) for generating the style of business plan that was on that board. This is the electronic version of the same idea.

Ultimately, success in any business is about creating value, not about being buzzword compliant. So sites that make fun of the buzzwords provide a very useful reminder.
