# Thomas Jefferson as Inventor and Maker

## Full text

[Makezine](http://www.makezine.com) has a nice Presidents' Day entry celebrating [Thomas Jefferson as an inventor and maker](http://blog.makezine.com/archive/2008/02/happy_presidents_day_from.html). Among the inventions called out: 

> "Moldboard of least resistance" - using math, Jefferson designed a better plow to lift and turn over sod more effectively - this new plow had a huge impact and instead of patenting the invention, Jefferson gave it away it - to be "solely used for the good of the people and not for the advancement of the inventor, Jefferson encouraged public use of this easily duplicated invention".
