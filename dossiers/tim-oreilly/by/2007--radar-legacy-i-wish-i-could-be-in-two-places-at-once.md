# I wish I could be in two places at once

## Full text

We've got Foo Camp starting in Sebastopol tomorrow, or else I'd be at [The Virtual Goods Summit](http://www.vgsummit.com/). It looks fascinating, with a great cast of presenters and an absolutely right-on premise: that virtual economies are for real, growing significantly, and will have a huge impact on our future world. Far more of what we pay for today is already more virtual than we realize.
