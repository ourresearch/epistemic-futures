# Maker Faire tomorrow and Sunday!

## Full text

Just a reminder: the [Maker Faire](http://www.makezine.com/faire) starts tomorrow at the San Mateo Fairgrounds. This should be an amazing event (science fair and DIY expo meets Burning Man), and I can't wait to [see what the Make team has put together](http://www.flickr.com/photos/tags/makerfaire/interesting/)! While the [weather](http://www.weather.com/weather/weekend/USCA1005) doesn't promise to be as good as it was earlier this week, it's not looking too bad for the weekend. Even if it rains a bit (40% chance), there's plenty to see that's under cover. See you there! (Apologies to all you non-local folks, but I imagine there will be boatloads of coverage online.)
