# A big spike for The Big Switch

## Full text

We saw an amazing spike in the [Bookscan sales data](http://radar.oreilly.com/archives/2005/04/book-sales-as-a-technology-tre.html) for Nicholas Carr's [The Big Switch](http://www.nicholasgcarr.com/bigswitch/) (a business book about the switch to utility or cloud computing) this past week. 

[](http://radar.oreilly.com/bigswitch.png)

We were curious about this huge jump. We see some recent [activity in the blogosphere](http://blogsearch.google.com/blogsearch?client=safari&rls=en&q=big+switch&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&um=1&sa=N&tab=nb), but nothing jumps out at us as driving this spike. Perhaps there was a publisher promotion? But if anyone knows of recent internet activity that could have driven a spike of this magnitude, we'd love to learn about it. (We're always interested in learning about online sources that drive book sales :-)
