# Why I Love Hackers

## Full text

TechWeb TV posted a short video from my opening keynote at [ETech](http://conferences.oreilly.com/etech08). Nominally this year's version of my O'Reilly Radar talk, which focuses on emerging trends that we're watching, the talk was wrapped in a larger theme, namely, why I love hackers, the edges they explore, and why hackers and alpha geeks, not entrepreneurs, are the first step in technology innovation. The video contains a short segment from the opening of the talk, and the closing. 

I hope to have the video of the entire talk available soon.
