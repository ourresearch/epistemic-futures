# Disney Pooh Camp

## Full text

Mike Pusateri of Disney just sent me this email message: 

> "Yesterday we held our version of Foo Camp for internal Disney people. The Pooh Camp was a great success and will become an ongoing event. Thanks for your ideas and advice. You both really helped convince the organizing group that a conference like this could work. I am in your debt. Here are [a few photos](http://www.flickr.com/photos/argyle/sets/72057594088579781/)." 

I'm glad to see that the [FOO Camp](http://www.google.com/search?client=safari&rls=en&q=Foo+camp&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8) style of conference worked for Disney. It's a great idea to bring people together for unstructured conferences, and I appreciate the way Disney reached out for advice.
