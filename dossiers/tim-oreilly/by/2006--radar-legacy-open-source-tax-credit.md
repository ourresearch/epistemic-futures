# Open Source Tax Credit?

## Full text

Carl Malamud wrote in email: "Thought this [open source tax credit](http://thinkprogress.org/2006/03/17/open-source-tax-credit/) initiative might be of interest to you. You might remember this proposal surfacing 8 years ago under the label "hacker tax credit." [Salon wrote about it in 1998](http://archive.salon.com/21st/log/1998/12/15log.html). Here's the [original proposal](http://public.resource.org/main.html).

If at first you don't succeed? :) This is getting serious attention on the hill, but some agitation from the net would help the senators and congressmen we're talking to see that this would be a good thing politically.
