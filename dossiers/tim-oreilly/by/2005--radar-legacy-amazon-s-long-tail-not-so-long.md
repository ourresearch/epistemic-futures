# Amazon's Long Tail Not So Long

## Full text

Chris Anderson, whose [original "Long Tail" article in Wired](http://www.wired.com/wired/archive/12.10/tail.html) claimed that Amazon gained a remarkable 57% of its sales from books not available in brick and mortar stores, now [retracts that number](http://longtail.typepad.com/the_long_tail/2005/08/a_methodology_f.html). Based on a recalibration, working with Amazon rank maven Morris Rosenthal, he now estimates the Amazon tail to be between 20 and 36%. Still a nice part of Amazon's revenue, but not as shocking as originally estimated.
