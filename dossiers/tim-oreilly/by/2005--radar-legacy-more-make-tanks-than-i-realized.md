# More "Make Tanks" Than I Realized

## Full text

A while back, in conversation with Danny Hillis, I suggested that [Applied Minds](http://www.appliedminds.com) might be called a "Make Tank", in a riff on the more traditional "Think Tank." A make tank is a lab that figures out how to design and build hardware that solves interesting new problems. [Squid-Labs](http://www.squid-labs.com), which has partnered with O'Reilly's [Make](http://www.makezine.com) on a number of projects, is another. But here at the [Where 2.0 conference](http://conferences.oreillynet.com/where), I learned of yet another: [Moto Labs](http://www.moto.com). You might think of [Inventables](http://www.inventables.com) as another type of Make tank. And as Nat pointed out to me, there are academic Make tanks, like [Neil Gershenfeld](http://web.media.mit.edu/~neilg/)'s lab at MIT, or [Tom Igoe's](http://itp.tisch.nyu.edu/object/IgoeT.html) at NYU.
