# Ads the reason movie theater revenue declining?

## Full text

[The Big Picture](http://bigpicture.typepad.com) argues that [the rise of advertising in movie theaters](http://bigpicture.typepad.com/comments/2005/07/declining_movie.html) has so adversely impacted the movie-going experience that it's one hidden source of declining movie ticket receipts. Worth reading.
