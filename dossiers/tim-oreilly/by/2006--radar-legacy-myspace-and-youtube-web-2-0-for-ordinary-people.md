# MySpace and YouTube:  Web 2.0 for Ordinary People

## Full text

Paul Boutin makes [a compelling case](http://www.slate.com/id/2140635/) that YouTube and MySpace show what it takes to bring Web 2.0 to ordinary people: "The secret to success is to make everything one-button easy, then get out of the way." He makes a few overblown points, claiming for example that Flickr has plateaued at a level not much higher than Slate (completely missing the logarithmic nature of the Alexa graphs on which he makes that assertion.) But his overall thesis is strong.
