# New Microsoft RSS Extensions

## Full text

I sent around to my editors a link to the [slashdot story on new Microsoft RSS extensions](http://slashdot.org/article.pl?sid=05/06/24/183213). John Osborn wrote back: 

> Interviews with the team responsible for the work -- now named the Longhorn Browsing and RSS Team, formerly the IE team -- [can be found on Channel 9](http://channel9.msdn.com/ShowPost.aspx?PostID=80533). The extensions will be released under the Creative Commons "share alike with attribution" license. 

Looks like the Channel 9 posting has some video demos and lots of discussion, but no downloads yet. Note the move by Microsoft to adopting [Creative Commons](http://www.creativecommons.org) licenses rather than rolling their own new ones. A lot of other companies could learn from that behavior!
