# Open Source, Web 2.0, and the Flat World

## Full text

If you're in NY, you might be interested in an event that I'm going to be part of on Nov. 30, along with Brian Behlendorf and Thomas Friedman, [Innovation and Growth in a Flat World](http://www.flatworldcmpcollabnet.com/). It's put on by [Collab.Net](http://www.collab.net) and [Information Week](http://informationweek.com/), and focuses on the challenges and opportunities of what Friedman has called "the Flat world." Space is limited; if you're interested, [apply for a ticket here](http://www.flatworldcmpcollabnet.com/). I'm looking forward to this. Brian is just about the most thoughtful guy there is about open source, and I've long been an admirer of Friedman.
