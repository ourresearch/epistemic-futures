# Wordle visualization of my Tweetstream

## Full text

[Mike Hendrickson](http://radar.oreilly.com/mikeh/) ([@mikehatora](http://twitter.com/mikehatora)) sent me a nice [Wordle](http://www.wordle.net/) visualization of my tweet stream, dating back as far as Twitter keeps it. As you can see, I retweet a lot. It's also interesting that many of the repeated words are not concepts or topics, but people's names (in the form of twitter handles.) This is one of the interesting things about twitter: it's a reflection of a community of shared minds, rather than of shared ideas.
