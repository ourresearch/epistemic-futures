# And These Guys Are Writing Our Laws?

## Full text

Having just discovered the [27B Stroke 6](http://blog.wired.com/27BStroke6) blog via the previous posting, I have to point to the latest entry as well, a hilarious reposting of [a shockingly ignorant speech on the net neutrality debate](http://blog.wired.com/27BStroke6/index.blog?entry_id=1512499) by Senator Ted Stevens (R-Alaska). You won't know whether to laugh or cry.

Update: Ryan Singel followed up with [an even funnier spoof](http://blog.wired.com/27BStroke6/index.blog?entry_id=1513010) of "the internet" that Stevens' staff supposedly sent to him that was delayed because of too much commercial activity filling up the Internet's tubes. When you're done with crying over the original posting, turn to the second one for a really hearty laugh.
