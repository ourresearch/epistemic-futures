# Google Talk APIs Released

## Full text

The march to ubiquity and innovation in IP telephony continues, with [Google Talk APIs](http://code.google.com/apis/talk/index.html) released. Much as happened with our [Where 2.0 conference](http://conferences.oreillynet.com/where/) (where Google Earth, Microsoft Virtual Earth, and Google and Yahoo! local APIs showed up just in time for the conference), the technology announcements are lining up in advance of our [Emerging Telephony Conference](http://conferences.oreillynet.com/etel). It's not that folks are targeting our conference, it's just that we put ourselves in front of an oncoming train...
