## Coverage notes & frontier

### What was searched (2026-08-13 pass)

**anildash.com — exhausted for the in-window period.** The site's own `/posts/` archive page renders
all 3,117 posts (1999-07-20 → 2026-07-24) with dates, titles and tags in one HTML document; that was
parsed into `blog-post-index.tsv` and cross-checked against `sitemap.xml` (identical count). All 1,151
in-window posts on the live site were downloaded and converted to individual full-text item files.
A Wayback CDX enumeration of `anildash.com/*` (20,971 URLs) was then diffed against the live archive
after normalising the several permalink schemes the site has used (underscore vs. hyphen slugs, `/amp/`
variants, and off-by-one-day permalink dates from an earlier migration). That diff surfaced **19 posts
dated 2005+ that now 404 on the live site**; 17 were recovered from Wayback and added. The two that
were not are `2007/09/17/ten_nine_eleven_things` and `2013/10/11/you_cant_spell_questlove_without_love`
— both snapshots exist and were fetched, but the post bodies are nothing but a PodTech player embed and
a Storify iframe respectively, i.e. there is no prose to save.

**anildash.com/press/** is the richest single hub: 33 podcast guest appearances, 6 "In the Press"
profiles/interviews, 5 outside bylines and a YouTube talks playlist. Every item on it was probed.

**Outlet author pages** checked directly: Wired (`/author/anil-dash/` — 6 pieces, all fetched, and the
listing does not paginate), The Atlantic (1), Rolling Stone (1), The Verge (no author page).

**Glitch / Fog Creek company blog** (`blog.glitch.com`, which now also serves the old `glitch.com/culture`

Anil Dash's byline** and are in `by/`.

**official full transcripts**. `glitch.com/culture/function-episode-N/` is offline, so all of them came
from Wayback: **18 numbered episodes plus the Texas Tribune Festival bonus are in `by/`** as
`talk-transcript`. Episode 5 has no usable snapshot. Six further episodes in the feed (the two Prince

### Notes on labelling decisions in this corpus

- Podcast interviews where the show publishes an official transcript are filed in `by/` per the blessed
  "voice is the payload" rule — typed `talk-transcript` when the transcript is the artifact and
  `interview` when the outlet published a Q&A article. Machine-generated transcripts (TWiT, Developer
  Tea) are kept with the generation method flagged in `notes:`.
- The 44 Glitch/Fog Creek company-blog posts under his byline are typed `blog-post` with the corporate
  context noted; they are his words, written as CEO.
- Items recovered from Wayback carry the snapshot timestamp and archive URL in `notes:`. Medium items
  additionally note that Medium UI chrome was stripped.
- Two anildash.com items (`2006--r-kellys-happy-people.md`, `2018--a-murmuration-of-starlings.md`) are
  one-line photo/video posts. `content: full-text` is accurate; they are simply trivial.
- Wayback `id_` responses are sometimes **gzip bytes with no `Content-Encoding` header** — the same
  serving quirk already recorded for content.openalex.org. The extraction pipeline sniffs `\x1f\x8b`
  and gunzips; without that, several Medium recoveries silently extracted as binary garbage.
