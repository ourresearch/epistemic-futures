# "V" is for "Kawaii"!

## Full text

Hey, how come Japanese folks always throw up the two-fingered “V” sign when you’re taking photos? Turns out it’s [because of an American figure skater’s activism](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V_sign#Japan_and_the_V_sign>). At least in part. (Don’t worry, it’s [still insulting](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V_sign#The_V_sign_as_an_insult>) elsewhere.)
