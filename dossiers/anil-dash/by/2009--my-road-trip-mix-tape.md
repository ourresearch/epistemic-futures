# My road-trip mix tape

## Full text

[](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=Janet+Jackson+On+and+On&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20> "Grab this Song from Amazon")

[On and On](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=Janet+Jackson+On+and+On&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20>) by [Janet Jackson](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=Janet+Jackson&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20>)

It's literally a song about cruising down the road with the top down.

[Dolphin](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=Prince+Dolphin&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20> "Grab this Song from Amazon") by [Prince](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=Prince&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20> "More from this Artist on Amazon")

It's about reincarnating as a dolphin, which is what I do on the road.

[](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=N.E.R.D.+Rock+Star&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20> "Grab this Song from Amazon")

[Rock Star](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=N.E.R.D.+Rock+Star&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20>) by [N.E.R.D.](<http://www.amazon.com/gp/search?ie=UTF8&keywords=N.E.R.D.&index=digital-music&tag=plinky09-20>)

I remember when I first got this album, visiting my parents and going to pick up my then-girlfriend, now-wife at the airport so she could meet the folks for the first time, and being excited and a little nervous and just rocking out with this song blasting out of the windows of the rental car.
