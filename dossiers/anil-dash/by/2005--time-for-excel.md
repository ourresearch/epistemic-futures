# Time for Excel Nerding!

## Full text

If you enjoyed posts such as [Excel Pile](</2004/04/22/excel-pile>), where people detailed their favorite ways of geeking out with Microsoft Excel, you may enjoy such recent discoveries such as [the drum machine worksheet](<http://www.milezero.org/index.cgi/music/tools/excel/drumpad_xls_alpha.html>) as well as the [LiveJournal posting client](<http://brad.livejournal.com/2185677.html>). Both are implemented as Excel worksheets. Both are ridiculously nerdy.
