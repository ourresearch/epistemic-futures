# The Amen Break

## Full text

Nate Harrison’s 2004 documentary [_Can I Get an Amen?_](<http://nkhstudio.com/pages/popup_amen.html>) on the “Amen Break”, uncovers the story behind one of the seminal breaks of the electronica/jungle/drum-n-bass scenes. Though I’d argue that James Brown’s “Funky Drummer” break is even more influential, and that the Kraftwerk/Africa Bambaata breaks are equally seminal in other genres, this is a fantastic demonstration of open culture and the profound ways that sampling can have an impact that no one could predict.
