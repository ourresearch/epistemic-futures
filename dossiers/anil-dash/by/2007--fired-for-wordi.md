# Fired for Wording!

## Full text

Sure, Microsoft Word is fine for kids who want to write papers for school, but serious professionals should be _very_ worried about using this dangerous tool! Just a few weeks ago, I found out about [this poor Des Moines woman](<http://cbs4denver.com/watercooler/watercooler_story_020113911.html>) fired for Wording at work! **Three hundred single-spaced pages** of irresponsibility. Her name is Emmalee Bauer, so perhaps we should give her a verb — be careful with your word processor, or you could get _Emmaleed_ ™.
