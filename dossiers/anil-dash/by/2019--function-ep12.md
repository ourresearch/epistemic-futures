Coding education for kids is wildly popular right now. From books and toys to after school programs from non-profit organizations, companies are putting lots of money and resources behind helping create the next generation of programmers. But is that enough? A few years ago, [Anil wrote about his skepticism behind these efforts](<https://anildash.com/2016/09/14/its_more_than_just_teach_kids_to_code/>), wondering if perhaps they are missing the mark on teaching proficiency over literacy.

On our season finale of Function, Anil sits down with **Alex Klein** , co-founder and CEO of [Kano Computing](<https://kano.me/uso>), a company that creates kits for all ages that help make coding and computing skills as simple and fun as putting together LEGO bricks. During their wide-ranging conversation (recorded at this year's [CES](<https://www.ces.tech/>)), learn about what motivated Alex to empower kids to create technology in this way, and hear his thoughts on the current trends around teaching kids to code.
