# Function with Anil Dash, ep. 16: American as Apple Pie: How Racism Gets Baked into Technology

## Full text

### "In the context of criminal justice, our past forms of racial profiling and policing get encoded in new systems because that history forms the dataset, the training data that then is used to make judgements." — Dr. Ruha Benjamin  
  
[](<https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/function-with-anil-dash/id1439658455?ls=1>)[](<https://www.google.com/podcasts?feed=aHR0cDovL2ZlZWRzLmZlZWRidXJuZXIuY29tL0Z1bmN0aW9uV2l0aEFuaWxEYXNo>)[](<https://www.stitcher.com/podcast/vox/function-with-anil-dash>)[](<https://open.spotify.com/show/61FfAOZJBJQbP7vfLoTuYB?si=9VICmm7DRDuiU-GfoNsj3Q>)

###  [](<https://overcast.fm/itunes1439658455/function-with-anil-dash>)[](<https://pca.st/gSE3>)[](<https://www.breaker.audio/function-with-anil-dash>)[](<https://feeds.feedburner.com/FunctionWithAnilDash>)

Anil speaks with [Dr. Ruha Benjamin](<https://www.ruhabenjamin.com/>), Associate Professor in the Department of African American Studies at Princeton University and author of [_Race After Technology_](<http://politybooks.com/bookdetail/?isbn=9781509526390>), about design discrimination. They discuss how systemic racism is replicated in the technology we use and how tools like artificial intelligence, machine learning and software used in the criminal justice system are shaped by racial bias.

Then he speaks with [James Cadogan](<https://www.arnoldventures.org/people/james-cadogan/>), VP of Criminal Justice, and [Kristin Bechtel](<https://www.arnoldventures.org/people/kristin-bechtel-ph-d/>), Director of Criminal Justice Research, of Arnold Ventures, a non-profit that funds the [Public Safety Assessment](<https://www.psapretrial.org/>), a tool used by judges that predicts a person's likelihood to reoffend or return to court if released.
