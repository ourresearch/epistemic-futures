# Nomenclature suggestion

## Full text

I think individual [user scripts](<http://dunck.us/collab/GreaseMonkeyUserScripts>) for [Greasemonkey](<http://www.nivi.com/blog/article/greasemonkey-and-business-models/>) should be called “[Smart Tags](<http://www.alistapart.com/articles/smarttags/>)“.

Everyone who switched sides on this one since 2001, please raise your hand. (For once, I get to smugly be consistent on this one.) **Update:** Or, you know, [GreasemonkIE](<http://www.daishar.com/blog/archives/2005/03/greasemonkey_fo.html>).
