# Depressing

## Full text

I was really depressed by the comments on [this post that Robert Scoble made](<https://web.archive.org/web/20050420002504/http://radio.weblogs.com/0001011/2005/04/17.html#a9890>) until I read the comment by [Mobile Jones](<http://www.mobilejones.com/>), who I think stated the point perfectly.

Until people stop being defensive, I’ll comfort myself with the thought that making the assumption that a market or conversation ought to be exclusionary is its own punishment.
