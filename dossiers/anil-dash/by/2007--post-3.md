# Personal Pies

## Full text

[](<http://www.flipflopflyin.com/personalpies/index.html>) Infographics and Peanut Butter week at dashes.com continues, this time with a terrific set of pie charts from Craig Robinson. You might know Craig as creator of [Flip Flop Flyin’](<http://www.flipflopflyin.com/>), whom we’ve loved for ages as creator of [minipops](<http://www.flipflopflyin.com/minipops/index.html>).

He’s done something great, though, with [Personal Pies](<http://www.flipflopflyin.com/personalpies/index.html>). Besides being entertaining, it’s doomed me forever to being cursed to think “That Flip Flop Flyin’ guy rubs ’em out to Madonna”. So there’s that.
