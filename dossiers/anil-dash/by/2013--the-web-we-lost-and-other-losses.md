# The Web We Lost, and Other Losses

## Full text

I got the chance to revisit some of the themes of [the Web We Lost](</2012/12/13/the-web-we-lost/>) in the broader context of how we [confront our mortality and impermanence](<https://www.wnycstudios.org/podcasts/notetoself/episodes/281706-planning-for-your-digital-life-after-death>) in the digital realm [on WNYC](<https://www.wnycstudios.org/podcasts/notetoself/articles/287072-tech-writer-anil-dash-web-we-lost>) a few weeks ago. I’m pleased with how the conversation came out, and if you’ve got 15 minutes, you can listen to it here.
