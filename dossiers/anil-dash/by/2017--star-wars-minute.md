# Star Wars Minute!

## Full text

In advance of the upcoming release of _The Last Jedi_ , excitement is building for all things Star Wars, so I’m thrilled to share that I got to be a guest on the inestimable Star Wars Minute podcast. The show tackles the world’s most popular franchise one movie at a time, one _minute_ at a time, and I was lucky enough to get to comment on everybody’s second-to-least-favorite Star Wars movie,_Attack of the Clones_.

It was really a joy to be on Star Wars Minute, and every one of these short conversations was amusing the whole way through — please do give them a listen!
