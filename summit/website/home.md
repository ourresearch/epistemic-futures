# The Epistemic Futures Summit: Sustaining Human Knowledge in the Age of AI

*Source: https://epistemicfuturessummit.pubpub.org/ — captured 2026-08-15*

## Workshop Description

The Epistemic Futures Summit is an invitation-only workshop bringing together scholars, technologists, publishers, and open-knowledge leaders to rethink how human knowledge can be sustained, curated, and trusted in the age of AI. At a time when traditional epistemic institutions are strained and AI systems are reshaping how knowledge and beliefs are produced and processed, we will map the emerging knowledge ecology and develop practical strategies to support its resilience and integrity. The Summit is also intended to be a design opportunity: a rare chance to bring the right people together to invent the mechanisms, standards, and institutions that the next era of knowledge will need.

## Target Outputs

- **An epistemic futures manifesto**: short (1,500–2,500 words), opinionated document of principles. Drafted within 2 weeks of the event by a writing committee of 3–4 participants. Not a consensus document; should include noted dissents.
- **An intervention roadmap**: 6–10 specific, actionable proposals with named responsible actors (funders, institutions, platforms, publishers). Each intervention gets one page: problem, proposed action, who acts, timeline, dependencies.
- **Open research questions**: curated list of 10–15 research questions the summit identified as critical and unresolved. Circulated to funding bodies as a potential research agenda.
- **Summary for funders and institutional leaders**: a 5-page briefing document designed to be handed to university presidents, foundation program officers, and policy makers.
- **Public signal**: a short public statement or op-ed that announces the effort without revealing deliberation details; preserves the off-the-record nature while creating public accountability.
